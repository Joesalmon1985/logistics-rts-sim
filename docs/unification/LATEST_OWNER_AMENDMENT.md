## 1. Direct owner direction

The owner wants NPCs to choose what to build and whether to fight, steal or retaliate, with Hawk/Dove-like strategies emerging from behaviour. The founding NPCs must be trained to sufficiently competent behaviour to survive. The owner's latest clarification is:

> To be clear, I don't want ownership as something 'hard coded' into the game. NPCs can decide to place goods somewhere, where they can be used communally. They may not share the location of goods they have, or decide to trade them etc, but really all a trade is is giving an NPC something on the understanding they will do the same (this is done through the communication system of offer and promise etc etc).

The earlier 12 controls, 12 needs, three-tab workbook, six tribes (7 adults + 5 children each), seven terrains, infinite food forage, dice-generated raws, building-gated raw extraction, labour-only first collection shelters, persistent individuals and god-only player remain. No new primitive verbs are needed.

## 2. Construction: knowledge and physical work, not economic scripts

- Represent known construction designs as brain knowledge with stable catalogue IDs, not necessarily portable blueprints. `USE` with a known design and a reachable/valid location (or explicit site marker) places an unfinished site. Knowledge supplies the design argument; it does not teleport or create goods. A failed attempt provides observable feedback. Define the site selection input in the same shared controller available to a possessing player.
- `USE` carried material on site contributes it; `USE` empty hand or appropriate tool on site advances time-based labour. Support cooperation, interruption, abandonment, resumption and partial progress. Finish only after required materials and labour; level-1 collection shelters require time/labour only. Upgrades, roads and settlements use the same pattern, with Catan placement rules and real work. No road flags or abstract carriers; roads connect existing settlements (planned routes may be proposals, not completed roads to nowhere; resolve earlier conflicting suggestion explicitly).
- Finished buildings expose physical capabilities, storage, occupancy and furnishings. Building placement, condition, reach, carrying and storage capacity are physical constraints. Record founders and contributors as provenance, not title. A structure may be used by any physically able NPC unless a physical barrier prevents access. Do not assign the builder perpetual privilege, automatically share goods or spawn a profession.
- The brain chooses which design, where, when and whether to cooperate or abandon it, using needs, observations, experience, knowledge and learned sequences. No fixed tech/build order, scripted shelter goal or omniscient selection of the best site. A labelled teacher may demonstrate during training, never drive the evaluation population. Generic bounded brain-internal planning is permitted over known interactions; it must not issue hidden high-level economic actions.

## 3. Physical possession is not ownership

Authoritative state: stable object/lot identity, quantity, condition, physical holder/location (ground, carry, equipped, container, construction site), physical barriers, production/consumption/transfer history, occupancy and labour. A holder is not a legal owner. Do not create canonical rightful owner, universal `UseRight`, owner-based candidate filtering, automatic ownership changes or automatic theft classifications in unified mode. Provenance is objective history, not proof of entitlement. Keep legacy Owner/UseRight fields only if needed for legacy serialization/regression, neutral in unified physical logic; audit every reader/writer, including indirect candidate filters and valuation. No silent adapter that recreates title or access control.

NPCs may form competing beliefs about entitlement, conceal locations, claim use of a bed, share a place, defend possessions, or change their minds. A claim can be represented as a local belief or a communicated/recorded proposition with claimant, subject, source, confidence, time and expiry; contradictory claims are valid. An observer must not query canonical truth about ownership. A communal hall is simply a shared place with a founding community's learned expectations, not an automatically owned asset or membership-gated store. Groups can make and communicate agreements but have no magical power over goods. Physical obstacles, occupancy, capacity, locked barriers (if implemented), and actual bodily resistance may obstruct taking or use; a belief or permission flag alone may not.

Natural raw extraction still requires a completed compatible collection building and capacity to deposit the output, plus geographical zone, physical accessibility, labour, carry capacity and available material. No permission check or ownership prerequisite. A physical temporary capacity reservation may prevent overbooking, but cannot confer exclusive title; use bounded expiry and account for interruptions. On expiry/full store/destruction, retain goods where physically located, allow another appropriate accessible destination or drop; no deletion or teleport. Picking up an already collected log is not extraction. A dropped lot retains identity/provenance, not legal title. On death, drop or physically retain goods according to their location; no universal inheritance or automatic title transfer. Family claims, gifts and succession are learned/social processes. Replace the existing deceased-owner invariant with location/conservation invariants, preserving historical regression tests in legacy mode. Audit all asset/store/estate/household/organisation paths, not just the collection API.

## 4. Communication and reciprocal giving

A trade is not a primitive action or atomic transaction. An offer and promise are communicated via GESTURE and referents (quantity/object/person); any transfer uses the same generic physical handover as a gift. A receiver's cooperation may be necessary to receive a carried good; no automatic reciprocal transfer or forced consent. Once A gives B wood, B may give promised stone, give a different quantity, delay, refuse, forget, or keep both. Record each actual transfer independently. An optional protocol identifier correlates messages and transfers for memory/analytics; it must not lock goods, grant title, settle debt automatically or create a claim that the physics enforces. Agreements are subjective and may be misunderstood. Observer beliefs and reputation update from perception or communication, not global ledger truth. Employment, loans, rations and taxation are later social interpretations of separate transfers and labour; retain useful existing social hooks only after removing mandatory reciprocity/ownership filters. Actual physically installed barriers or people can enforce conventions by their actions. No auto enforcement by state/market/contract.

## 5. Conflict, disputes and retaliation

Implement a small persistent-NPC physical conflict slice with existing controls: `USE` empty hand or a compatible held object on another reachable NPC can attempt a strike; `USE` protective equipment on self equips it; step and turn allow retreat/pursuit; gesture can signal threat, submission or protest; remedies can be used on an injured person. Effects are abstract game mechanics: reach, elapsed work, fatigue, injury, pain, fear, condition and mortality. No faction-level military AI or spawned abstract soldiers. Same-tribe and cross-tribe encounters follow the same physical rules. No guaranteed hit or forced retaliation. Keep impact, victim and observer identity distinct. Record observed injury, transfers, claims, responses and communications with provenance. A theft is someone's interpretation of a `TAKE`, not an engine offence. A dispute may arise despite accurate observation. Unseen actions must not reveal the perpetrator to the victim. No mandatory aggression, automatic punishment, instant omniscient reputation or global moral score. Hawk, Dove, Retaliator, Bully, reciprocal cooperator and cheat are measured patterns, not policy classes in the island. Keep exact game-theory lab separate. Later courts, policing and institutional law are possible NPC behaviours; do not implement them as required outcomes tomorrow.

## 6. Train the founders before claiming an autonomous society

UN6t is on the critical path. Train against the authoritative simulator and same twelve controls, observations, needs and catalogue; offline training and versioned deterministic inference separate from live simulation. Curriculum: motor control and exploration; food/water/sleep; hygiene/comfort/social/fun; use/store/withdraw; selecting and completing useful construction; raw collection and simple recipes; offer/promise/handover and non-reciprocation; aggression/avoidance/retaliation, contested goods, childcare and observation. Use demonstrations/associative learning or RL as appropriate; do not require a specific ML framework. Use local information and physical legality masks only (not masks of what is socially allowed or optimal). Feedback comes from experienced needs, pain, material access, risk and relationships, not reward for specific professions, civilisational milestones, morality or winning a dispute. Longer-horizon credit and sequence memory are needed. Different adults may have different trained experience and skills. Children inherit biological architecture and temperament, not adult personal memory or acquired knowledge; model teaching and observation, with matched exposure/no-exposure tests.

Before attaching trained policies as default: record policy and content hash, seed split, config, curriculum, artifact, training metrics and held-out evaluation. Set numerical survival/competence thresholds before seeing held-out results; include untrained/frozen comparison, no teacher or god, unfamiliar layouts, interrupted tasks and no hidden path oracle. Separate basic survival competence from experimental social outcomes. Do not require Hawk/Dove proportions, perfect cooperation or guaranteed long-run viability. If training fails, keep the result, flag any fallback visibly, do not mark learned UA passed. Do not mistake a scripted demonstration or a primitive controller wrapped around the old `Eat/Flee/Build` rules for training. Protect save/load/replay, fixed ordering, bounded memory and C# inference parity.
