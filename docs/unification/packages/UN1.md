## 1. Objective
Build the unified world on the UN0 foundation: seven terrains and a sea ring (54-hex default, radius configurable, spiked first), coast nodes; infinite time-limited forage places (seven foods, rabbit at three times the time) and herbs; fresh-water points and salt water (§D9.5 G3); the dice allowance with **gradual materialisation** on raw nodes including sea fish; the environment books; the rates and decay of the seven new need channels; and the effect handlers `Forage` and `DrinkWash`. All world queries go through `ISpatialIndex`.

## 2. User-facing outcome
Headless: the scenario `unif_world` shows a board of seven terrain types inside a sea ring, forage and water reachable from every start corner, dice rolls turning into raw stock that appears one unit at a time on raw nodes, and the seven new need channels moving at their configured rates. Nothing eats, collects or builds by itself yet (no brain, no buildings): the demonstration drives the effect handlers from fixtures. Unity presentation is UN10a's.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **Board spike first** (merge gate, index row 3): a unified board generator hunk in `Board/BoardGenerator.cs` (flagged, additive, behind `Unified.On`): `HexTypes.OfTerrain` (Forest, Mountain, Wetland (from `Hills`), Pastures, Fields, Desert, Sea (from `Meadow` rim and the ring)); `BoardGenerator.UnifiedPool` (30 land hexes by default: 5 Forest, 5 Mountain, 5 Wetland, 6 Pastures, 6 Fields, 3 Desert; configurable in `UnifiedTuning.UN1`); sea ring from `Board.Radius` and `Board.SeaRing` (default radius 4, one ring: **the 54-hex figure is 30 land hexes plus the 24-hex ring; reconcile with the 61-hex disc and report, section 18**). `HexType[]` lives in `UnifiedState` (UN1 partial), not in `Board`, so the Stage 3 `Board` and its hash are untouched. Number tokens 2-12 are dealt to **every non-desert hex including sea** (D9.12), deterministically from `Rng.Fork("unified-numbers")`, so each hex has production. Spike deliverable: a test that radius 4 (the default island) and radius 6 generate valid boards (UC scale) with at least three settlement corners per tribe site available at radius 6; commit and tell the lead before building on it.
2. **Coast nodes:** a walkable land node adjacent to a Sea hex (design §3.3 and §2.2). Sea hexes and sea-only nodes are impassable (expose through `ISpatialIndex.Walkable`). No boat, raft or swimming.
3. **Forage places** (`ForagePlace { Id, Node, Ware, Ticks }`, 23.8): deterministic placement from `Rng.Fork("forageplaces", hex)` (`ForagePlacesPerHex` default 4, at least two nodes apart, none within 2 nodes of a start corner's hall site), one food per hex type: Forest `Mushroom` 72, Mountain `Roots` 29, Wetland `WildEgg` 73, Pastures `Rabbit` 74 (three times the base time; one edible portion, `EdibleRaw` 1, an abstraction of catching and preparing, G9), Fields `WildVeg` 75, Desert `CactusFruit` 76, Sea `Seaweed` 77 on **coast nodes only**; herbs (`Herbs` 32) as an infinite time-limited forage-like place on Mountain and Forest hexes (G5). Defaults from design §3.2 (nutrition 120/200/180/350/160/100/90, ticks 30/45/40/90/30/40/35, spoil days 2/6/4/1/3/3/2); all configurable in `UnifiedTuning.UN1`. **Forage never depletes and no dice, building or tool is involved** (B1). Legacy `ResourceSource` objects are not placed in unified mode (`Ecology.Place` is skipped by UN0's start seam; `PopulationState.Sources` empty).
4. **Effect handler `Forage` (EffectKind 1):** at completion of `USE` empty hand on a `ForagePlace` it calls `Goods.Produce(..., ProduceReason.Foraged)` creating one lot in the actor's hand/carry (fails `NoRoom` if the hand/carry cannot take it, never drops it silently), emits `ForageCollected` 1200. **`DrinkWash` (EffectKind 2):** `USE` empty hand on a `WaterPoint`, slot `drink` or `wash` (20 ticks default): drink lowers Thirst and needs a `Fresh` point (salt water returns `NoInteraction` for drink), wash lowers Hygiene (fresh or salt); **no lot is created and no container is needed** (G3); emits `WaterUsed` 1204. Eating is UN3b's `Eat`.
5. **Water points** (`WaterPoint { Id, Node, Fresh, Building }`): every wetland land node is a fresh point; one spring node per Forest, Mountain, Pastures and Fields hex (placed by the generator; deterministic); coast nodes carry salt water (wash only); Desert has none. `World.AddWaterPoint(...)` and `World.RemoveWaterPoint(...)` are the API UN3b calls for the Well (`Building` field) and UN4 for destruction.
6. **Dice allowance and gradual materialisation (§D9.1 b 7, design §4):** because `DiceSystem` is skipped, UN1 owns a unified dice clock in `TickWorld`: every `DiceIntervalTicks` it rolls 2d6 from the **same keyed stream** `Rng.Fork("dice", RollCount)` with its own `RollCount` in `UnifiedState.World` (so totals equal Stage 3's for the same seed); a 7 does nothing; every non-desert hex whose number matches gets `AllowancePerRoll` (default 6; sheep in head, default 2) added to `HexSupply.Pending` (cap `AllowanceCap`, excess to `Wasted`, `AllowanceWasted` 1202; `AllowanceGranted` 1201); every `MaterialiseIntervalTicks` (default 30) each hex moves `MaterialiseUnits` (default 1) from `Pending` to a `RawNode` of that hex (lowest `Count`, ties lowest id; a full node waits). `RawNode { Id, Hex, Node, Ware, Stock, Cap }`: `NodesPerHex` default 4 deterministic nodes per raw hex (spacing at least 4 nodes, none within 2 nodes of a hall site), sea hexes use coast nodes for `Fish` 30. Raw wares: Forest `Log` 1, Mountain `Stone` 5, Wetland `Clay` 2, Pastures `Sheep` 79, Fields `Hay` 78, Sea `Fish` 30; Desert none. **Raw nodes are not lots and cannot be picked up;** the extraction gate (completed collection building, reservation, carry) is UN3b's `CollectRaw`, which calls `World.TakeRaw(node, n)` (UN1's API; the only way `Stock` decreases). `RawMaterialised` 1203. Environment books `Geo-1` to `Geo-5` (design §4.6) as invariants.
7. **Needs (G15, `Unified/Needs/*`):** rates and decay of the seven channels in `UnifiedNpc.Needs[7]` (Thirst, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun, Comfort, Room) in a `NeedsConfig` (per-day rates, age and temperament variation bounded, relief magnitudes consumed by UN3b effects), updated from `TickWorld` for persons with a unified record; the five drives are untouched. `Needs.ActiveMask` (12 bits, default 0xFFF): an inactive channel is held at 0, excluded from the encoder and from health. **Health coupling (§D9.4 d):** only Hunger, Thirst, Warmth (exposure) and injury affect health; the other channels affect comfort, mood and reward only. Thirst health loss goes through the existing health mechanism that hunger uses in `PopulationSystem.Body` (read it first); if no callable entry exists, file an amendment request, do not edit `PopulationSystem`. `Needs.Apply(s, npc, channel, delta)` is the public API UN3b and UN8a call. Reachable relief per active channel is verified by UN3b (NEED-1); UN1 provides the audit helper `World.ReachAudit` (BFS in tests only, never in a decision path) that checks every start corner has fresh water and a forage place within `StartReachMaxEdges` (default 30).
8. **World queries** only through `ISpatialIndex` (UN0 interface): UN1 supplies `World.*` data providers (entities at node, nodes in radius, walkable, class lists); the simple implementation is UN2's.
9. Scenario `DotNet/God.Runner/Scenarios/unif_world.scenario.json` and runner support to run it (`Unification.Enabled = 1`, no population behaviour required).

## 6. Explicitly excluded scope
**D9 rules, binding on every line of this package** (amendment §§1-5, §D9.1, contracts §23):
- **No title, no permission.** The world records holder, location, quantity, condition, occupancy, labour and objective history; never a rightful owner, use right, membership gate or theft. No `.Owner`, `OwnerRelation`, `Permits.`, `StoreAccess`, `RightOn`, `Truth(` reference anywhere under `Unified/` or in a unified branch of a shared file (U-P3 static guard). A founder or contributor is provenance only. No adapter recreates title or access control; a claim is a brain belief, never world state.
- **No high-level verbs.** The only actions are the twelve `Prim` controls through `Motor.Submit`. No new learning `Verb`, `NpcVerb`, `MaxCand` change, goal table, `Build`/`Gather`/`Trade` action or `MoveTo` for an NPC; construction, collection, exchange and conflict are interaction effects reached by `USE`, `TAKE`, `DROP` and `GESTURE`.
- **No scripted behaviour.** No build order, shelter goal, profession, role, reciprocity, retaliation or "best site" selection in the game. A scripted teacher exists only in `DotNet/God.Train` and test fixtures, labelled `ControlOrigin.Teacher` or `Fixture`, and never drives an evaluation population.
- **Physical gates only.** A control may fail only for reach, inputs, capacity, weight, occupancy, passability, a physical barrier, time or a known-design requirement (`NotKnown` is a legality fact of the brain's knowledge, not a permission). A belief, claim, group, household, tribe or legacy owner field never blocks anything (EP-01).
- **One executor, origin-neutral.** Policy, possession, avatar, compulsion, fixtures and the teacher submit the same `PrimitiveControl`; origin is recorded on events and never changes a physical result (QA-01, U-P4).
- **Legacy untouched.** `Unification.Enabled = 0` is byte-identical to the base (U-P1). Legacy Stage 3, M3-M5 and C1-C7 code stays compiled and pinned; unified code never reads legacy ownership or permission paths (audit §8). Emit nothing rather than delete events.
- **Scale (D10).** Nothing is sized by tribe count, board size or population; every such number is configuration (23.1). Perception and witnesses go through `ISpatialIndex`; cost is O(active persons + changed entities) per tick; no all-pairs scan, no per-tick allocation on hot paths, no `Dictionary` iteration or wall clock; integers and keyed `Rng` forks with labels unique per call site.

Specific to UN1: no collection building, reservation or deposit (UN3b); no eating, relief objects, hearth, well building or latrine (UN3b); no executor, USE resolver, perception encoder or step logic (UN2); no brain; no start population, halls or founders (UN5); no construction sites (UN4); no catalogue content beyond what tests need (UN3a owns the rows; UN1 tests use code-defined test rows); no road or settlement logic (UB1); no boats, deep-sea fish or finite fish; no change to Stage 3 dice, piles or `ResourceSource` code (they stay for legacy); no edit to `Board.cs` or the Stage 3 `Board` arrays; **never a raw ware produced except through UN3b's collection** (`Geo-4`); no shared-throughput forage cells (design §3.3.4 stays off and unimplemented).

## 16. Performance considerations
Scale rule (D10): world generation is O(hexes + nodes of interest); `TickWorld` is O(hexes with pending allowance + persons with a unified record) per tick, never all-pairs; place and node lists are sorted arrays, lookups by id binary search or direct index; no `Dictionary` iteration; no allocation per tick; needs update touches only living persons. Budget: world generation at radius 6 under 200 ms; `TickWorld` at 1,200 persons under 0.5 ms per tick (report measured values). Per-node `ForagePlace` and `RawNode` counts are bounded by `ForagePlacesPerHex` and `NodesPerHex` so observation lists stay small.
