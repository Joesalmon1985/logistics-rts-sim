## 1. Objective
Construction as physical work reached by `USE`: a **known design** plus `USE` on a valid placement creates an unfinished **construction site** (`Sites.Place`); `USE` carried material on the site delivers it (`Contribute`); `USE` empty hand or a tool adds time-based labour (`Labour`, tool multipliers); anyone may contribute, cooperate, interrupt, abandon and resume; completion needs the required materials **and** labour and then creates a building (`Buildings.Create`); upgrades L2 and L3 run as upgrade sites while the building keeps operating; damage and destruction drop contents. Founders and contributors are provenance, never title. Delivers EC-01 and the site parts of EP-05.

## 2. User-facing outcome
Headless: a person who knows a design places a site on a legal node; people (the same or others, in any order, at any time) deliver materials and labour until the site completes and becomes a building; a level-1 collection shelter needs only labour (600 ticks default) and produces nothing before completion; a site left alone stays as it is and can be resumed by anyone. A possessing player (UN10a later) uses the same controls. There is no builder privilege and no scripted shelter goal: whether, what, where and with whom to build is the brain's choice.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **`Sites.Place` (EffectKind 12, `PlaceSite`):** `USE` with `PrimitiveControl.Design` = the `CatId` of a design the actor **knows** (`Knowledge.Knows`, else `NotKnown`) on `TargetKind.Placement` (`Id` = node, optional facing) or the node the actor faces. Valid only if the `BuildingDef` placement rule holds: land, terrain rule of the row (fisher's shelter on a coast node, shepherd's on pasture, and so on, from UN3a data), no building or site on the footprint or its neighbours, inside the development area where the row requires it (production buildings and L2+ upgrades; in UA the six founding settlements' areas exist from the start; seam `UnifiedHooks.DevelopmentArea`, default true until UN5 fills it), within reach of the actor. A failed attempt gives **observable feedback**: `ControlFailed` with `BadSite`, `NotKnown`, `Occupied`, `Blocked` or `OutOfReach`, and `SitePlaceFailed` 1241 with the reason. Success creates a `ConstructionSite` (`Design`, `Level`, `Node`, `Facing`, `Bin` of kind `Site`, `LabourNeed` from the level row, `Founder` = the placer as provenance, `Placed` tick) and emits `SitePlaced` 1240. **No goods are created or teleported by placing.** `Sites.Legal(s, actor, design, node)` is the pure query behind `Motor.Legal` (the `LegalPlacement` seam), so a possessing player and the brain use the same site-selection input.
2. **`Contribute` (13):** `USE` a carried lot on a site moves it (`MoveReason.Contribute`) into the site bin, only wares the design still needs and only up to the requirement (`Full` otherwise: U-C6, site bin contents at most the requirement); records a `Contribution { Npc, Labour, Ware, Units, Last }` (bounded 16 per site and building: merge by person, evict the oldest when full; provenance only, no rights). `MaterialContributed` 1242 (HV).
3. **`Labour` (14):** `USE` empty hand or an appropriate tool (source requirement from the row) on a site is one short repeatable **application** (default 20 ticks; a stone hammer shortens its duration by the data multiplier 0.7 through the resolver, never gates it): at completion `LabourDone` rises by the nominal unit; refused with `Full` when `LabourDone` already equals `LabourNeed` (nothing to add; materials are missing). Labour may run ahead of materials; many people may work the same site at once (no cap; ascending actor id order within a tick). `LabourContributed` 1243 (HV). An interrupted application loses only its unfinished unit; completed units persist (interruption, abandonment and resumption are physical).
4. **Completion:** when `LabourDone >= LabourNeed` **and** the site bin holds every required material, the site completes in the same tick of the last contribution: required materials are consumed with `Goods.Consume(ConsumeReason.Built)` **only now**, `Buildings.Create` (UN3b) creates the building at the node with `Founder` and `Contributors` copied as provenance and `Completed` = tick, the site is removed (id never reused), `BuildingCompleted` 1244. **Level-1 collection shelters need labour only (no materials)** and produce nothing before completion.
5. **Upgrades (L2, L3):** `PlaceSite` on an existing building of the same design family with the next level creates an upgrade site (`UpgradeOf` = building id, `Level` = target); at most one active upgrade site per building; the building keeps operating at its current level; materials and labour come from the next level row; completion calls `Buildings.SetLevel` (storage and sleeping spots change; supply does not), `UpgradeSitePlaced` 1245, `UpgradeCompleted` 1246.
6. **Abandonment and resumption:** a site with no work for any time is left exactly as it is (`SiteStaleDays` 0 = never decays; configurable); anyone may resume it, regardless of who placed it or contributed (no knowledge of the design is needed to *work* a site, only to place one); no automatic destruction, no ownership transfer, no recovery of delivered goods other than by destruction drops below.
7. **Damage and destruction API:** `Construction.Damage(s, ev, building or site, amount, cause)` lowers `Condition` (`BuildingDamaged` 1247); at 0 the building (or site) is destroyed (`BuildingDestroyed` 1248): all bin contents (storage, fixtures, workstation, site) drop to the ground at its node through `Goods` with `SiteContentsDropped` 1249, occupants are released, reservations are cancelled (goods stay where they are), `Buildings.Remove`, and a Well's water point is removed (`World.RemoveWaterPoint`). The API is used by fixtures, divine miracles and UB conflict; **no UA system calls it by itself** (no decay in UA unless `SiteStaleDays > 0`). No deletion of goods, ever (U-C4).
8. **Settlements and roads are not built here.** `PlaceSite` accepts only UA designs; staged rows (`Settlement`, `Road`, UB shelters) return `Disabled`. UN4 shapes `Sites` generically so UB1 reuses the same pattern (settlement and road sites with real materials and labour, roads between existing settlements only).
9. **Unified tick slot** `UnifiedHooks.UN4.cs`: `TickSites` (stale-site bookkeeping when `SiteStaleDays > 0`, completion checks if deferred); registers the three handlers with `EffectRegistry`; fills `LegalPlacement`.
10. Invariants: extend U-C6 for sites (`Site_NoOverRequirement`); `Sites_NoOrphanBins` (every `Site` bin belongs to a live site; destruction empties it).
