## 1. Objective
Turn the owner's workbook into a reviewed, versioned, deterministic content source and generated runtime tables: `docs/unification/catalogue/catalogue.json` with **all** 12 controls, 12 needs, 138 objects and 65 buildings (id, `catId`, name, aliases, workbook row, workbook SHA-256, stage, fix codes, status, reason), the 39 UA-initial rows with their corrections applied and the §D9.5 numbers; a dependency-free generator and validator `DotNet/God.Catalogue`; generated C# tables; the closure, cycle and reachability audits; and the CATALOG-1 coverage report. After this merge, UN3b, UN4, UN5 and UN6t read content from here and never hand-write recipes.

## 2. User-facing outcome
A reviewer can open one JSON file and one report and see, for every workbook row, its stable id, its stage (UA-initial, UB, Later, Superseded), which D9 corrections apply, and whether it is implemented, staged, deferred or rejected, with the reason. The game gets deterministic generated tables (`ObjectDef`, `BuildingDef`, `RecipeDef`, `InteractionDef`) for the UA content: the six collection shelters (L1-L3), hall, hearth, well, carpenter, latrine, the seven forages, herbs, cooked fish, planks, bedding, bed, stool, bench, toy, ornament, chest, cradle, waste, stone hammer and axe.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **`docs/unification/catalogue/catalogue.json`** (the reviewed source of truth; the workbook is the authoring backlog): 12 controls and 12 needs as in the workbook; 138 objects (`OBJ-nnn`, `catId` 1000+nnn) and 65 buildings (`BLD-nnn`, `catId` 2000+nnn) with fields `id`, `catId`, `name`, `aliases`, `workbookRow`, `workbookSha256`, `stage` (`UA-initial`, `UB`, `Later`, `Superseded`), `fix` (the codes of manifest §5 that apply), `status` (`implemented`, `staged`, `deferred`, `rejected`), `reason`, and for active rows the definition data. Ids are sheet order at the pinned hash and **immutable**; renames add an alias. An initial import reads the workbook with a one-off generator command (`God.Catalogue import-workbook`), verifies the SHA-256, joins with the manifest tables (parse them; a test fails on any mismatch of id, name, workbook row or stage) and is then reviewed by a human diff.
2. **Corrections applied to the 39 UA-initial rows** (29 objects, 10 buildings; manifest §3.3): fix codes N1, A-I per manifest §5 and the §D9.5 resolutions: no rights, no ownership or membership wording; offers are gestures; no consent flag; explicit slots (water `drink`/`wash`; buildings `deposit`, `install`, `work`, `fuel`, `cook`); tools are duration multipliers (stone axe 0.6 wood collection, stone hammer 0.7 construction labour, stone pick 0.7 stone collection) and strike-capable (attack 2, empty hand 1); alias normalisation (bedding mat to Sleeping mat, target ball to Hoop, jump platform to Low jump platform, cool storage to Cool storage chest, fire watch to Fire warning station, painting board to easel, sundial to Sun marker, water basin to Water feature); alternatives as separate recipes; TBD costs stay `TBD` and inactive; L1 shelters have basic sleeping spots only (furnishings are craft options, G14).
3. **UA content numbers (§D9.4 b, §D9.5):** six L1 collection shelters (labour 600 ticks, no materials, storage 10, 2 sleeping spots, collection radius 2 rings), L2 (10 wood + 300 ticks, storage 30, 4 spots), L3 (20 wood + 10 stone + 600 ticks, storage 60, 6 spots); Communal hall BLD-001 (founder building, storage 40, 12 sleeping spots, integral hearth capability, fixture slots for 12 mats, 2 benches, 1 chest; endowment is UN5's); Primitive hearth (1 stone, 120 ticks; fish and wood inputs, slot *cook* 60 ticks gives cooked fish; fuel 1 wood per 1,200 ticks; warms within 2 nodes); Well (4 wood, 300 ticks; creates a fresh-water point); Carpenter (3 wood, 1 stone; planks, bedding, bed, stool, bench, toy, carved ornament, storage chest, cradle, latrine as separate recipes with the manifest's inputs); Latrine (2 wood + 1 clay; stores 20 Waste, slot *relieve*); stone hammer and stone axe (wood + stone). Forage rows (seven foods, herbs) and water point rows reference UN1's places and handlers (`Forage`, `DrinkWash`). Every number is a configurable default recorded in the file.
4. **`InteractionDef` rows** for every UA object and building: `{ TargetKind, TargetCat, Slot 0-3, SourceReq (EmptyHand | Ware | Class | Tool), Reach, Ticks, Effects[] }` using only `EffectKind` 1-20 (Forage, DrinkWash, Eat, NeedRelief, CollectRaw, Deposit, Install, Occupy, WorkInput, RecipeWork, Fuel, PlaceSite, Contribute, Labour, HoldOut, Strike, Equip, Treat, Relieve, Play). Needs relief amounts (the 12 channels in the order Hunger, Energy, Warmth, Safety, Pain, Thirst, Hygiene, Bladder, Social, Fun, Comfort, Room) are `EffectDef` arguments, with at least one reachable relief row for every channel that has one in the manifest (Hunger: forage and cooked fish; Thirst: water; Energy: bed, hall, shelter floor; Comfort: stool, bench, bed; Warmth: hearth, shelter; Hygiene: water; Bladder: latrine; Room: carved ornament, chest, waste handling; Fun: toy; Pain: herbs (`Treat`); Social and Safety have no catalogue relief row (gestures, shared play, movement) and the report says so).
5. **`DotNet/God.Catalogue`** (net8, no external dependency): commands `import-workbook`, `generate`, `validate`, `report`. `generate` emits `Assets/God/Sim/Unified/Catalogue/Generated/*.g.cs` (deterministic: no timestamps, fixed ordering by `CatId`, LF line endings; a partial of the UN0 `Catalogue` facade; a constant `CatalogueHash`, the SHA-256 of the canonical active-row JSON, which UN6t's artifact header records), plus `docs/unification/catalogue/catalogue.csv` (a stable export for UN11a's dimension table). `validate` runs the audits below.
6. **Audits:** *closure* (every UA input is UA, forage or a dice raw, or the founder endowment), *cycle without bootstrap* (no recipe cycle lacking a bootstrap path), *reachability* (every UA object is producible from labour-only L1 shelters, forage, dice raws and the endowment; mines, metals and deposits are marked Later with "no bootstrap path", G10), *id and alias integrity*, *effect-kind validity* (only kinds 1-20, slots 0-3, args in range), *no ownership wording* (no active row text or field names owner, permission, right, recruit, employ, consent; any UA row with an unresolved fix code fails).
7. **CATALOG-1 report** `docs/unification/catalogue/CATALOG1_REPORT.md`: row-by-row status for 12 controls, 12 needs, 138 objects and 65 buildings; active vs defined needs; recipe and interaction coverage; unresolved bootstrap dependencies (reed, seeds, mines); the list of rows needing owner or lead decisions. Update the status columns of `CATALOGUE_MANIFEST.md` (the only existing file edited besides the catalogue folder).
8. **Ware ids:** UA needs only the fixed wares 72-93 and reused ids (§23.4). Any object needing a ware beyond them (UB rows) is recorded as `staged` with the requirement for ware ids 94-159 (allocated in manifest order, append only); **UN3a does not edit `Economy/Wares.cs`** (frozen): it lists the requested names for the lead.

## 6. Explicitly excluded scope
**D9 rules, binding on every line of this package** (amendment §§1-5, §D9.1, contracts §23):
- **No title, no permission.** The world records holder, location, quantity, condition, occupancy, labour and objective history; never a rightful owner, use right, membership gate or theft. No `.Owner`, `OwnerRelation`, `Permits.`, `StoreAccess`, `RightOn`, `Truth(` reference anywhere under `Unified/` or in a unified branch of a shared file (U-P3 static guard). A founder or contributor is provenance only. No adapter recreates title or access control; a claim is a brain belief, never world state.
- **No high-level verbs.** The only actions are the twelve `Prim` controls through `Motor.Submit`. No new learning `Verb`, `NpcVerb`, `MaxCand` change, goal table, `Build`/`Gather`/`Trade` action or `MoveTo` for an NPC; construction, collection, exchange and conflict are interaction effects reached by `USE`, `TAKE`, `DROP` and `GESTURE`.
- **No scripted behaviour.** No build order, shelter goal, profession, role, reciprocity, retaliation or "best site" selection in the game. A scripted teacher exists only in `DotNet/God.Train` and test fixtures, labelled `ControlOrigin.Teacher` or `Fixture`, and never drives an evaluation population.
- **Physical gates only.** A control may fail only for reach, inputs, capacity, weight, occupancy, passability, a physical barrier, time or a known-design requirement (`NotKnown` is a legality fact of the brain's knowledge, not a permission). A belief, claim, group, household, tribe or legacy owner field never blocks anything (EP-01).
- **One executor, origin-neutral.** Policy, possession, avatar, compulsion, fixtures and the teacher submit the same `PrimitiveControl`; origin is recorded on events and never changes a physical result (QA-01, U-P4).
- **Legacy untouched.** `Unification.Enabled = 0` is byte-identical to the base (U-P1). Legacy Stage 3, M3-M5 and C1-C7 code stays compiled and pinned; unified code never reads legacy ownership or permission paths (audit §8). Emit nothing rather than delete events.
- **Scale (D10).** Nothing is sized by tribe count, board size or population; every such number is configuration (23.1). Perception and witnesses go through `ISpatialIndex`; cost is O(active persons + changed entities) per tick; no all-pairs scan, no per-tick allocation on hot paths, no `Dictionary` iteration or wall clock; integers and keyed `Rng` forks with labels unique per call site.

Specific to UN3a: no runtime behaviour, no effect handlers, no resolver, no building or site logic (UN3b, UN4, UN2); no editing of the workbook; no Excel or OpenXML dependency at run time (the generator reads the workbook once, then `catalogue.json` is the source); no active content for UB or Later rows (they stay `staged`/`deferred` with reasons); no TBD cost activated; no new runtime catalogue other than the generated tables; no ownership, permission, membership, profession or consent field in any definition; no change to `Wares.cs`, `EffectKind` or `CatalogueDefs` (frozen; an amendment request if the schema cannot express a row); no road or settlement content before UB1 (rows stay `staged`).

## 18. Risks
- **Reading the workbook without a dependency.** An xlsx is zipped XML (shared strings, sheets); handle merged and multi-line cells ("some cells hold several recipe lines") and the 138/65 row counts with a golden check, not guesses.
- **Silent semantic drift.** A correction mistakenly left un-applied would put ownership or consent wording into active content; the NoOwnershipWording and fix-code tests are the guard. Numbers in the workbook are provisional defaults, not owner facts.
- **Ware ids 94-159.** `Economy/Wares.cs` is frozen; UB2 and any UB row needing a ware wait for a lead-run edit. Report the requested names; do not edit the enum.
- **Schema fit.** If `InteractionDef`/`EffectDef` cannot express a UA row (for example a recipe with two alternative lines or a by-product like Waste), file an amendment request; do not extend the schema.
- **Gaps recorded, not decided:** reed and seeds (G7), mines (G10), Meal/Ration (G12) stay UB/Later; Social and Safety relief have no catalogue row by design.
- **Determinism of the generator** across machines (line endings, culture-sensitive formatting): use invariant culture and LF; the byte-identical test is run on the CI machine.
