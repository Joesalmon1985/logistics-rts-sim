## 1. Objective
The unified world is **seen**: one continuous landscape of seven geography types and a sea with no visible hex edges or node markers in normal play; collection shelters with their level, deposited goods, forage places and raw-material lots, the communal halls and settlements, roads, group work sites, and an inspection that explains who owns what, who may sleep and collect, what each person's current goal and group are. The view only reads sim state, sends commands and animates events.

## 2. User-facing outcome
The player starts the unified game and watches six tribes live on a believable island: forest, mountain, wetland, pasture, field, desert and a sea coast blend into one landscape; people forage along the shore and under trees, build small shelters that grow into huts and workshops, carry wood to stores, sleep inside, walk faster on roads people built together, and found a settlement at the edge. Selecting a person or building shows the facts.

## 5. Allowed scope
**UN10a (landscape, forage, raw lots, shelters, boot, inspection):**
1. **Continuous landscape** driven by `Board.HexType`: one ground blend per type (forest floor, mountain rock, wetland mud and reeds, pasture grass, field, desert sand, sea) with soft boundaries between hexes through the existing `IslandField`/ground mesh pipeline (a per-vertex blend of the nearest types; no per-hex tiles); a sea surface and shoreline with a shader boundary; dressing density per type (trees, rocks, reeds, grasses, cacti, kelp); **no hex edges, no node markers, no flags in normal play** (the dev overlay `DevOverlays` still shows them behind a key).
2. **Forage and raw-material presentation**: forage places hinted by the dressing (mushrooms under trees, shore weed, cactus fruit) with a small visual cue on use; raw nodes (`RawNode`) drawn as piles in the type's material (logs, stone, clay, hay, a flock of sheep, fish at the shore) sized by quantised count and updating as the sim materialises or takes them; carried loads visible on the NPC.
3. **Buildings**: original, simple procedural or modelled shelters for the six kinds and the communal hall, with **three level looks** (primitive lean-to, hut, workshop), a visible store (stacked goods count), sleeper indicators, condition (ruin) and construction progress; a distinct (owner-colour-free) look: ownership shown in inspection, not by tribe colour; tribe colour only on people and the hall banner. No Sims-2-derived or third-party assets.
4. **Boot**: the unified start (UN9b) renders without the Stage 3 views; the camera frames the island and the six halls; `SmokeRun` batch smoke shows no exceptions.
5. **Inspection and HUD** (registered through the `GodHud` hook, no edits to frozen HUD files beyond the registration): a **building card** (kind, level, owner, rights: collection, sleeping, storage, goods ownership, store fill, beds used, condition, upgrade state, who works there), a **person card additions** (current goal and step, shelter used, buildings owned, group memberships, knowledge bits as names, forage skills), a **geography tooltip** (hex type, forage food, raw material, current allowance state if the god cheats the debug view), and a small **economy summary** (counts of buildings by level, total foraged and collected today).
6. **Events to animation**: `ForageCollected`, `CollectionDeposited`, `BuildingCompleted`, `UpgradeCompleted`, `BuildingSlept` drive small sounds-free animations and toasts through the existing `ToastFeed`; no new audio.

**UN10b (settlements, roads, groups, overlays):**
7. **Settlements and areas**: a settlement marker (a small standing stone or gathering of shelters, original) and an optional **development-area overlay** (soft ring; dev toggle, and shown when the player selects a settlement); planned sites as a faint stake.
8. **Roads**: built road nodes drawn as a worn path blending into the ground (width and wear by use); unbuilt prefix of a project drawn as marked stakes; road projects show contributors' work visibly (people on the segment).
9. **Groups**: a group work site flag (`ObjClass.GroupSite`) at the project; the inspection shows members, contributions, free riders (analytics layer, labelled as the god's knowledge) and the objective.
10. **Overlays for analysis** (dev/god toggles): hex types and numbers, raw-node allowance state, travel heat map (journeys per node: where a road would pay), specialisation (activity colour per person), ownership map (building owner kind by tint); each reads sim state only.
11. **PlayMode tests**: boot, camera, inspection cards exist and populate on the fixture; screenshot capture in batch mode where the platform allows.
