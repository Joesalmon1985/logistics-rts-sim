# UN8: superseded (split into UN8a and UN8b under D9)

**Status: superseded on 28 Nov 2026 by owner decisions D9.1–D9.14.** The D8 "UN8 informal groups, group-owned assets and organisation hand-over" spec is withdrawn: groups own nothing and have no power over goods (D9.4, amendment §3), and no organisation is founded by a template (D9.1).

Read instead:
- [UN8a.md](UN8a.md): conflict slice (strike, equip, treat, witness-local fear, CF-01, CF-02 harness), Tier UA.
- [UN8b.md](UN8b.md): informal groups on D9 rules (proposal by gesture, voluntary joining, real contributions, group action as behaviour), Tier UB.
- Exchange and claims (the "claims, offers, promises, giving" part the owner called UN8a in the amendment) are [UN7.md](UN7.md).
