## 1. Objective
Make buildings and the data-defined interactions physical: the `Building` capability model (storage, sleeping spots, fixture slots, workstation input and output, condition, occupancy); the effect handlers `Eat`, `NeedRelief`, `CollectRaw` (the **physical** gate plus capacity reservations with expiry), `Deposit`, `Install`, `Occupy`, `WorkInput`, `RecipeWork`, `Fuel`, `Relieve`, `Play`; and the UA capabilities of hearth, well, carpenter, latrine and hall. Delivers EP-01 and EP-02 (mechanical), NEED-1 relief and ECON-1 (mechanical): shelter, collect, carpenter, stool or bed, install, occupy, measured benefit.

## 2. User-facing outcome
Headless: a completed collection shelter lets a person collect a raw unit only when a compatible shelter is within 2 rings and its store has room; the unit goes into the collector's carry with a bounded reservation, is deposited by a separate `USE`, and nothing is lost when the reservation lapses. A carpenter turns carried wood into a stool or bed that can be installed in a hall and occupied for a measurable comfort and energy benefit. Any able person may use any physically accessible store, bed or workstation: no owner, no permission, no veto.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **`Buildings` API (`Unified/Buildings/*`):** `Buildings.Create(s, ev, cat, level, node, facing, founder, contributors, completed)` allocates a `Building` (id from the `UnifiedState` counter, never reused) and its bins from the `BuildingDef` level row: `Storage` (BinKind 7, capacity), `Fixtures` (9, `FixtureSlots`), `WorkIn` (10) and `WorkOut` (11) for workstations, `Container` (12) inside an installed chest; `Buildings.SetLevel` (used by UN4's upgrade completion: storage and sleeping spots change while the building keeps operating); `Buildings.Remove` (UN4's destruction calls it after emptying bins). `Building` has **no owner, right or permission field**; `Founder` and `Contributors` are provenance copied from the site. `Condition` is physical: a building at 0 is ruined and its storage is `Blocked`; operational means `Condition >= MinOperational` (default 1). Creating a Well also calls `World.AddWaterPoint(node, fresh: true, building)`.
2. **`CollectRaw` (EffectKind 5), the physical gate (§D9.1 b 7):** `USE` empty hand (or a tool as source, duration multiplier only) on a `RawNode`, accepted only when (a) a **completed, operational** collection building whose `CollectWare` matches the node's ware lies within its collection radius (default 2 rings of nodes) of the node, (b) that building's storage can accept one more unit counting open reservations, (c) the actor can carry the unit (`NoRoom`/`NotEnough`), and (d) the node has stock. No permission, group or owner check exists. On completion: `World.TakeRaw(node, 1)`, `Goods.Produce(..., ProduceReason.Collected)` into the collector's carry, a `Reservation { Building, Npc, Units, Expires }` opened for `ReserveTicks` (default 600), `RawCollected` 1262, `ReservationOpened` 1264. **Picking up an already collected log is ordinary TAKE, not extraction.**
3. **`Deposit` (6):** `USE` carried lot on a building's slot *deposit* moves it with `Goods.Move(MoveReason.Deposit)` into `Storage`; it consumes the actor's reservation first if one exists; `Deposited` 1263. Capacity is physical (`Full`, `NoRoom`).
4. **Reservations (U-C6):** open reservations never exceed storage room, each carries an expiry (`ReserveTicks`), and the interaction tick `TickInteractions` expires them (`ReservationExpired` 1265). **On expiry, a full store or destruction the goods stay exactly where they physically are** (the collector's carry, the ground, the store): no deletion, no teleport; the collector may deposit elsewhere or drop. A reservation is a capacity hold, never title or exclusivity; two actors may both hold carried units.
5. **`Eat` (3):** `USE` a food lot (in hand/carry) on self consumes it (`ConsumeReason.Eaten`) and lowers Hunger by the `ObjectDef` nutrition; raw fish and raw meat have nutrition 0 and no `Eat` row (G2); cooked fish via the hearth. **`NeedRelief` (4):** generic relief of channel A by amount B (args from the catalogue), applied at completion, through `Needs.Apply`. **`Relieve` (19):** `USE` a latrine slot *relieve* (or any outdoor node) relieves Bladder; the latrine takes up to 20 `Waste` units internally (`ProduceReason.Excreted`, `WasteProduced` 1269); when full it behaves as an outdoor node; outdoors lowers Hygiene and Room for persons within a small radius (G8). **`Play` (20):** Fun relief from a toy; Social relief only when another person's `Play` is within `PlayNodes` in the last `PlayWindowTicks` (shared play needs a second person's actions, no consent flag).
6. **`Install` (7):** `USE` a carried furnishing lot on a building's slot *install* moves it (`MoveReason.Install`) into `Fixtures` up to `FixtureSlots`; the inverse is `USE` empty hand on the fixture, slot *uninstall* (`MoveReason.Uninstall`); `FixtureInstalled` 1250, `FixtureRemoved` 1251. Anyone may install or remove; capacity is the only limit.
7. **`Occupy` (8):** `USE` a sleeping spot (the building's basic spots plus installed beds and mats; one body per spot, first come first served) sets `UnifiedNpc.Occupying` and `Building.Occupants` (sorted, at most the spot count); `Occupied` when full; the occupant leaves on its next step, on a physical interruption or on death (physical, not a right); `OccupancyChanged` 1267. While occupying, `TickInteractions` applies the data-defined relief (Energy recovery, Comfort bonus from an installed bed or stool, Warmth near a lit hearth) through `Needs.Apply` and the existing sleep recovery; the **measured benefit** of ECON-1 is the difference against sleeping on the ground.
8. **`WorkInput` (9), `RecipeWork` (10), `Fuel` (11):** workstations (hearth, carpenter): `USE` a carried ware on the workstation slot *input* moves it into `WorkIn` (`MoveReason.WorkInput`); `USE` empty hand or tool on slot *work* or *cook* (the slot selects the recipe line; alternative lines are separate recipes) runs `LabourTicks`, and at completion consumes the inputs (`ConsumeReason.ProcessInput`) and produces the outputs into `WorkOut` (`ProduceReason.Crafted`); `RecipeCompleted` 1266; the output is taken by TAKE. Refusals: `NotEnough` (inputs absent), `Blocked` (not operational), `Busy`. `Fuel`: `USE` wood on the hearth adds fuel; a lit hearth burns 1 wood per 1,200 ticks and warms persons within 2 nodes. Recipes are catalogue rows (UN3a): a new recipe needs no new code. **No knowledge or consent flag gates a recipe** (manifest fix D); the brain's knowledge only shapes its own choices.
9. **Capabilities as data:** hearth, well, carpenter, latrine and hall behaviours are `BuildingDef` capabilities and `InteractionDef` rows (UN3a), not hard-coded building types; the hall is a shared place (12 sleeping spots, storage 40, hearth capability), not an owned asset or membership-gated store.
10. **Unified tick slot** `UnifiedHooks.UN3b.cs`: `TickInteractions` (reservation expiry, occupancy relief, hearth fuel burn and warmth, workstation progress if any is stored) and the emitters for 1260 `InteractionStarted` (HV) and 1261 `InteractionCompleted` (HV) behind UN0's `OnInteractionStarted/Completed` seams. Registers the effect handlers with `EffectRegistry` in fixed order.
11. **Invariants:** extend UN0's U-C3 and U-C6 for building bins, fixtures and reservations; a unified invariant `Building_NoOverCapacity`, sleepers at most spots (`Unif-6`), bin content at most capacity counting reservations (`Unif-5`).

## 6. Explicitly excluded scope
**D9 rules, binding on every line of this package** (amendment §§1-5, §D9.1, contracts §23):
- **No title, no permission.** The world records holder, location, quantity, condition, occupancy, labour and objective history; never a rightful owner, use right, membership gate or theft. No `.Owner`, `OwnerRelation`, `Permits.`, `StoreAccess`, `RightOn`, `Truth(` reference anywhere under `Unified/` or in a unified branch of a shared file (U-P3 static guard). A founder or contributor is provenance only. No adapter recreates title or access control; a claim is a brain belief, never world state.
- **No high-level verbs.** The only actions are the twelve `Prim` controls through `Motor.Submit`. No new learning `Verb`, `NpcVerb`, `MaxCand` change, goal table, `Build`/`Gather`/`Trade` action or `MoveTo` for an NPC; construction, collection, exchange and conflict are interaction effects reached by `USE`, `TAKE`, `DROP` and `GESTURE`.
- **No scripted behaviour.** No build order, shelter goal, profession, role, reciprocity, retaliation or "best site" selection in the game. A scripted teacher exists only in `DotNet/God.Train` and test fixtures, labelled `ControlOrigin.Teacher` or `Fixture`, and never drives an evaluation population.
- **Physical gates only.** A control may fail only for reach, inputs, capacity, weight, occupancy, passability, a physical barrier, time or a known-design requirement (`NotKnown` is a legality fact of the brain's knowledge, not a permission). A belief, claim, group, household, tribe or legacy owner field never blocks anything (EP-01).
- **One executor, origin-neutral.** Policy, possession, avatar, compulsion, fixtures and the teacher submit the same `PrimitiveControl`; origin is recorded on events and never changes a physical result (QA-01, U-P4).
- **Legacy untouched.** `Unification.Enabled = 0` is byte-identical to the base (U-P1). Legacy Stage 3, M3-M5 and C1-C7 code stays compiled and pinned; unified code never reads legacy ownership or permission paths (audit §8). Emit nothing rather than delete events.
- **Scale (D10).** Nothing is sized by tribe count, board size or population; every such number is configuration (23.1). Perception and witnesses go through `ISpatialIndex`; cost is O(active persons + changed entities) per tick; no all-pairs scan, no per-tick allocation on hot paths, no `Dictionary` iteration or wall clock; integers and keyed `Rng` forks with labels unique per call site.

Specific to UN3b: no construction sites, labour, contribution, placement or upgrade logic (UN4; UN3b only provides `Buildings.Create` and `SetLevel`); no damage or destruction logic (UN4); no world, forage, water, dice or raw materialisation (UN1; UN3b calls `World.TakeRaw`, `World.AddWaterPoint`, `Needs.Apply`); no catalogue content (UN3a); no executor or USE resolver (UN2); no hold-out, claims or exchange (UN7); no strike, equip or treat (UN8a); no brain; no founder endowment or hall placement (UN5); **no `.Owner`, `OwnerRelation`, `Permits`, `StoreAccess`, `UseRight`, access list, key, lock, membership or "may use" check anywhere** (a "physical barrier" is reach, capacity, occupancy, passability or a ruined building; locked barriers are not implemented in UA); no builder or founder privilege on bed, store or workstation; no automatic sharing, no profession or role; no new `ProduceReason`/`ConsumeReason`; no water as a lot or container (UB); no `Play` or Fun content beyond the toy (the cut plan may drop `Play` and `Fun`: record if so).

## 16. Performance considerations
Scale rule (D10): only active reservations, occupants, lit hearths and running workstations are processed per tick (small sorted lists, not a scan of all buildings); the collection gate finds nearby buildings through `ISpatialIndex` (radius query, not all buildings); occupancy and reservation lookups by id; no allocation per tick; building and bin counts are not capped by constants (a settlement of 100+ people must work). Budget (report measured): `TickInteractions` under 0.3 ms per tick at 200 buildings and 1,200 persons; `CollectRaw` validation O(buildings in radius).
