## 1. Objective
Prove, honestly and reproducibly, that the unified world behaves as designed and show what it actually does: the acceptance rows U1–U14 and B1–B11 as one scenario suite; analytical warehouse tables with exact reconciliation (the roll-to-lot chain and the goods ledger); deterministic replay; the **long-run demonstration** of D8.18 (leave the 72 people running with no intervention and report what emerged); and the multi-generational **viability experiments** on the new forage model. The pass condition of the demonstration is a **report**, not a preset outcome.

## 2. User-facing outcome
After a run the owner can open a report that says, per seed: when the first collection building, upgrade, exchange, recipe run, group, road, new settlement and organisation appeared (or that they did not), how many people lived and died of what, how work time split between foraging, building, collecting and producing, how unequal holdings became, and whether a replay reproduced it exactly. A SQLite file allows any question to be asked afterwards.
