## 1. Objective
Military organisations obtain equipment **through the same factories, labour, materials, fuel and power as civilians** (IMIL-1; D6.8: "no separate military economy"): ammunition and firearms are ordinary recipes on I1's runner, a military organisation gets them by **owning an arsenal** or by a **procurement agreement** (an I3 agreement of kind `Procurement`) with a factory owner whose office-holder places the orders, and a tank is a VEH vehicle of class `Tank` built, crewed by two employees and driven. **Infantry are existing people** (IMIL-2): nothing spawns a soldier. Combat, the strategic device and the rest of the OpenRA categories stay in the design (Tier 3).

## 2. User-facing outcome
In `ind_military`, a military organisation's commander agrees with the private factory to buy 20 rounds of ammunition and 4 firearms; the factory's manager queues them among civilian orders by its own priorities; the delivery makes them the military's and opens a debt; later a tank rolls out of the same factory that built the harvester, and two employed crew members board and drive it. The god may instruct the commander (who may refuse), compel a manager to place one order, or possess a tank driver; it cannot conjure crews, fuel or ammunition.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **Recipes** 33 (ammunition) and 34 (firearm) appended to `RecipeBook` content (I1 owns the runner and table code; you add rows through the documented append path and their envelope rows when K1 exists), plus tank and carrier assembly rows with `AssetOutput` = the new vehicle defs (numbers original, in `SimTuning.Industry.Military`).
2. **Vehicle defs** `Carrier` (PassengerSeats 6) and `Tank` (crew min 2: driver + gunner seat; `AmmoBin`; `Health`/`Armour` fields stored for Tier 3) appended to I2's def table.
3. **Military organisation layer** (`Industry/Military/*`, `IndustryState.Military`): the commander office (C6) decides procurement and orders from its own `InstitutionView`; budget lines are C6's; equipment **delivered** under a procurement agreement becomes the military organisation's (basis `SupplyAgreement`, one obligation, K2a payment if money exists); kit lots are lent to soldiers (existing store and lending rules); vehicles become C1 assets owned by `Org(military)`.
4. **Procurement agreements**: `Agreements.Make` (I3) with `Kind = Procurement`, item = a ware or a vehicle def with count; `ProcurementAgreed` mirrors I3's `SupplyAgreementMade`; the factory owner's office-holder places the matching orders (I1 verb) among civilian ones by its own priorities; `MilitaryEquipmentDelivered` per delivery. A military organisation that **owns** the factory (arsenal) places orders directly.
5. **Crewing** (IMIL §4.2): crew posts are assignments of employees (C3 contracts with role `Crew`) who board by their own choice under their job; `CrewPostFilled`/`CrewPostVacated`; a tank **moves** only with a living driver and holds a gunner seat; **no attack** in this package.
6. **Logistics**: fuel and ammunition reach depots and vehicles through I2 routines and `BinTransfer`; `AmmunitionExpendedDay` exists for Tier 3 (zero now).
7. **Competition measured**: IMIL-01/02/04 sources (military share of factory ticks, steel and fuel; readiness) in the day rows; never a rule.
8. Content block for Q3: `ind_military` (military organisation, commander, procurement agreement); your own scenario `DotNet/God.Runner/Scenarios/i5_procurement.scenario.json`.
9. Migration **`0035_military_industry.sql`** and `I5Mappings`.
