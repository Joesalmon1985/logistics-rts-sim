## 1. Objective
A **power station receives and consumes physical fuel**, and **electricity reaches a connected factory through built links**; cutting the fuel or a link stops powered production, and restoring it resumes (slice steps 5, 6, 9b, 9c; ACCEPTANCE D6.3, D6.4). Electricity is **neither a good nor money** (ELE-1, ELE-2, IA-5): it is a flow on a connected component during an interval, recorded in an **energy ledger**; a claim arises only from a person's billing act, revenue only from a payment. No faction power number (ELE-4), no central fuel market (ELE-7, D6.6), any ownership model (ELE-8), staffed stations (ELE-9).

## 2. User-facing outcome
In Iron Coast the public utility's coal station runs while three staff are on duty and coal lies in its fuel bin; its output reaches the smelter and the factory along power lines on poles. When the coal runs out (the harvester stopped, the coal agreement broken) or a lineman opens a switch, the factory bay goes dark and its queue stalls; restoring either resumes the same cycle. Once I6 lands, the electricity overlay shows components and energised lines, the station card shows staff, output and days of fuel left, and a flickering lamp marks every facility without power.

- **Staff absent at night**: stations go dark if no one is on duty; that is a real outcome, not a bug. Report it.
