## 1. Objective
The player **sees industry working inside the seamless island** (D6.19): deposits as outcrops and seams, facilities that animate only while their events say they work, harvesters moving between nodes with **visibly heaped cargo**, power lines, the facility, vehicle and power-station **inspection cards** (with the production queue), the **electricity overlay** and **shortage glyphs** — all without exposing the hex grid (D5.18), all read-only (the Game layer never mutates sim state). And the warehouse reports it: migration `0037` with the industrial KPI views (IND, VEH, ELE, WST, ENV, IMIL).

## 2. User-facing outcome
Checkpoint G's demo: the god watches a harvester crawl from the hill mine to the smelter with its ore heap visible, coal arrive at the station, a lit factory bay where a harvester frame grows with cycle progress, a named worker boarding the new vehicle; the player opens the facility, vehicle and station cards and the electricity overlay, then cuts a link by compelling a lineman (or lets the coal run out) and sees the outage glyphs and the stalled queue, then the recovery. The only actions offered on industrial cards are the existing divine ones aimed at the people involved (instruct, compel, possess, miracles); the god never places an order, sets a routine, a dispatch rank, a tariff or a budget.

## 5. Allowed scope
**I6-a (minimal, for Checkpoint G):**
1. **Deposits drawn** (`DepositSkinPlanner` + layer): outcrops and seams around the deposit node within `RadiusNodes`, feathered, never hex-shaped; only as the god observes (hidden deposits not drawn unless the profile shows them); extent non-increasing as `Remaining` falls; a spent deposit as a scarred pit.
2. **Facilities drawn** (`FacilityLookPlanner` + layer): distinct original silhouettes for mine, smelter, factory, power station (others later); smoke, sparks, lit windows and moving parts **only while the last status event is `Working`**; workers at the entrance from the facility's present workers; output heaps from the output bin; the factory bay shows a vehicle frame growing with cycle progress.
3. **Vehicles drawn** (`VehiclePosePlanner` + layer): original low-poly harvester and hauler, interpolated between `PrevNode` and `Node` from `VehicleMoved` at the step rate; cargo heap scaled by the cargo bin's weight share and coloured by ware; operator visible; exhaust only while moving; stopped-reason glyph (fuel can, spanner, empty seat).
4. **Power lines** along link polylines (subtle in normal play; slack when cut; open switch shown).
5. **Cards** registered through U1's registration hook (R9): **facility** (owner organisation and model, design, status and stall reason, production queue rows with recipe, quantity, done, progress of the running cycle, inputs and outputs by ware and owner, workers present, power share, condition), **vehicle** (owner, operator and crew, following the routine or not, routine step, cargo by ware and owner, tank level, condition, odometer, trips today), **power station** (owner, design, on/off, output and availability, staff on duty vs minimum, fuel bin and days of fuel left, ash bin, dispatch rank). Each datum carries its provenance tag (PLAYER_UI §12); the organisation's own record beside observation where one exists (§20). The only actions are existing divine ones on the people shown.
6. **Electricity overlay** (`GridOverlayPlanner`; off by default; toolbar Analysis tray or hotkey): links coloured by component, energised vs dark, station output and fuel bars, consumer rings (full, partial, out), outage spells highlighted; never reveals the hex grid.
7. **Shortage glyphs and alerts**: flickering lamp over `NoPower` facilities, empty bunker at stations short of fuel, empty input bays for `MissingInputs`; alert digest rows for outage start/end and a station with under a day of fuel, one card per category per day (§14 aggregation).
8. **LOD**: far silhouettes with status colour and vehicles as dots; mid models without cargo detail; close full detail.

**I6-b (Tier 3 and analytics):**
