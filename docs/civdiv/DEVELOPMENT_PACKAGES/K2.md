## 1. Objective
**One money and accounting architecture** (D6.2): no OpenRA credits, no Power Grid money, no external buyer, no sink. Money is either a **commodity money** — lots of a ware people accept in exchange, moved only by `Goods` — or (K2b) **institutional money**: claims on an explicitly defined issuer, created and destroyed **only** by the listed mechanisms E1–E4 and D1–D5 (MONEY §4.2). Every payment is **one act by a person** with a `Payment` record; organisations keep **double-entry books in native units**, posted only from payments and obligation changes; revenue exists only from actual payments by parties in the world. Production, extraction, refining, delivery, unloading, electricity generation or supply, construction, research, a completed order, a miracle and the passage of time never change a money balance and never create a claim (IA-2).

## 2. User-facing outcome
In Iron Coast people and organisations hold coins from the start (a seeded convention, listed as such); the smelter's paymaster walks to the miners' intake and hands over coins for delivered ore; the utility's treasurer reads the factory's meter and issues a bill, which the factory's clerk later pays at the utility's office. The organisation card shows revenue and expense by purpose and unit **as the organisation's records state them**, beside the truth books the god's observation allows. Coins visibly change hands; a payment is never a floating number. With K2b, a temple's tally store or a cooperative bank can found its own money and keep accounts.

## 5. Allowed scope
**K2a (Tier 2):**
1. **Seeded commodity money** (MONEY §3.2): when `Civ.Money.Enabled = 1`, the scenario's money block (contracts gap I1a item 17) seeds holdings of `SliceCommodity` (`Coin` in `ind_*`, else `Bronze`) for persons and organisations with `ProduceReason.Placed` (on the ledger from tick 0), each adult's price belief (`SocialMind.Price[coin]`), and each seeded organisation's unit of account; listed in the scenario and in `fact_scenario_seed`. After tick 0 the ordinary mechanics apply: people may stop accepting it, hoard it or barter. **No recipe or mint is required**; if one exists later, a coin is produced only by its own recipe (MON-I4).
2. **The `Payment` record and the one payment act** (`Civ/Money/Payments.cs`): `Payments.Begin` (only allocator of `PaymentId`), `Payments.Step`, completion and abandonment, exactly MONEY §5.1–§5.2: the authoriser (payer or its agent with `Spend`) moves `Amount` units with `Goods.Move` to the receiver's carry (`HandOver`, within `Reach.HandOver`) or the payee's bin (`Deposit`, within `Reach.Store`), `OwnerRule.Set` to the payee, `purposeRef = PaymentId`, basis by purpose (`Wage` 16, `Purchase` 23, `Levy` 19, `Repayment` 18, `Loan` 17, `Restitution`, `Offering`, `SupplyAgreement` 25 for supply deliveries, **`Paid` 26** for fees, fines, licence fees, royalties, auction settlements, energy bills, rent, grants, damages); several steps allowed (`PaymentStep` HV, `PaymentMade` at completion); either side may stop (`PaymentAbandoned` leaves an obligation reduced by exactly what was handed over); out of reach fails atomically (`PaymentFailed`, MON-U4). The act is **C3's `Pay` (101) generalised** to any `PaymentPurpose` (flagged additive edit to C3's file after C3, contracts §22.9). In-kind payments (a wage in bread, a bill paid in coal) are the same act with `Unit = Ware(w)`. Payer and payee are always existing Npc, Household or Org (MON-I5). A `CivState.K2.Payments` ring keeps the last `PaymentRing` entries (history is in events).
3. **Person-to-person M5 deals** stay as built; with `RecordPersonSales = 1` (industrial scenarios only) the consideration legs get `Payment` records; otherwise the warehouse books them from exchange events.
4. **Settlement of obligations**: payments that name a C2 obligation settle it by exactly `Paid`; obligations change only by payment, delivery, write-off, forfeiture or ruling (MON-I8).
5. **Organisation books** (`Civ/Money/Books.cs`): `OrgBook` per organisation that paid, was paid or holds an obligation (ascending OrgId, ≤ 48 `BookBalance { AccountClass, UnitRef, PurposeOrZero, Dr, Cr }`); **`Books.Post` is the only writer**, called only by `Payments`, (K2b) `Money`, and C2's obligation API on open, adjust and close; posting rules of MONEY §6.2 (native units, balanced per unit, memo `GoodsIn/GoodsOut` lines, `basis` cash or accrual); `OrgBookDay` at the `CivSystem` K2 day-start partial (ascending OrgId). **No decision-maker reads books** (BK-I4 reflection). **Records** (what the organisation knows) are written by its treasurer with `KeepRecords` as C6 `RecordEntry` rows when it pays, is paid or learns of a staff payment; records may differ from truth (measured). Persons' books are derived (`Books.PositionOf`, warehouse journal lines).
6. **Units of account and accepted units** as C6 policy rows (`UnitOfAccount`, `AcceptsInPayment`, `PaysWagesIn`, `TariffPer`, `PriceList`; flagged additive rows after C6): followed by staff by their own scores, never binding on buyers, no shared price table; `UnitOfAccountChanged`.
7. **The billing act** `IssueBill` (verb 127, `NpcVerb` 213): a supplier's office-holder, from what the organisation knows (a meter reading, a licensee's report, its own inspection), opens one C2 obligation of source `Bill`/`Royalty`/`Fee` and posts both books; **never on a timer from truth** (MONEY §7.1).
8. **C2 obligation sources and events** (flagged additive rows in C2 after C2 merges, contracts §22.9): sources `Supply`, `Bill`, `LicenceFee`, `Royalty`, `AuctionPrice`, `Damages`, `Tax`, `Fee`; the non-loan obligation events **1190–1194** (catalogued in your `CivEvents.K2`, emitted by C2's API); `Books.Post` hooks on open and close. Without C2 merged: spot payments only (no bills or deliveries on credit).
9. **Moneyness and `MediumOfExchangeObserved`** (ECONOMY §4.11): analytics in the warehouse plus the event when the measure exceeds 500‰ for 10 days (computed from events by the sim's day start only if cheap; otherwise warehouse-only, record which).
10. **Static guards with real code**: `NoExternalBuyer`, `MoneyWriters_OnlyK2` (only `Payments`, `Books` and, in K2b, `Money` write `CivState.K2`; no industrial module references them except to call `Payments.Begin/Step` from a person's act), BK-I4 reflection.
11. Content for Q3: the `ind_slice` coin holdings, price beliefs and units of account; your own scenario `DotNet/God.Runner/Scenarios/k2_payments.scenario.json`.
12. Migration **`0038_money_claims.sql`** and `K2Mappings`.

**K2b (Tier 3, after G):**
13. **Institutional money** (`Civ/Money/Money.cs`, `Accounts.cs`): `MoneyDef` (issuer `OrgId`, form, redeemability, reserve rule as a charter term checked by staff, status), founded only by the issuer's decision `FoundMoney` under a charter with `Purpose.Issue`; only an office-holder with the `Issue` power at the issuer's place records issuance or destruction; **E1–E4 and D1–D5 are the complete list**, each one `Money.Issue`/`Money.Destroy` call paired with its asset or liability change; accounts (one `Deposit` obligation per account at C2), transfers at the issuer by the clerk's act (`Payments.Transfer`, C2 `Adjust`), notes (lots plus the issuer's notes-outstanding liability).
