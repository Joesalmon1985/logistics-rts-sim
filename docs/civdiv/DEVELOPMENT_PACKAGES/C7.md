## 1. Objective
- **C7a healthcare:** an injured or ill person requests help and recovers measurably faster through another NPC's treatment (end-to-end scenario 5), informally or through a healer's organisation, with real time, reach and (optionally) a remedy good.
- **C7b education:** one NPC teaches another, with a **measurable capability change** in the learner (scenario 6): copied associations and a skill rise, which may carry the teacher's mistakes.

## 2. User-facing outcome
The player sees a wounded NPC gesture for help, a healer come and kneel beside it, and its health recover faster than an untreated neighbour's; a parent or teacher demonstrates gathering and the child later gathers better; the Mind tab shows new imitated associations. Treatments and teaching appear in the person card (data from the inspection cards, drawn by U5). Checkpoint E (with C6's store).

## 5. Allowed scope
**C7a healthcare:**
1. **Skill record (default per SERVICES §11 Q1):** `Skill.Healing` and the teaching-topic skills as a C7-owned table `OrgState.C7.Skills` (sorted by NpcId, `byte[]` per NPC for at most `Orgs.C7.SkillSlots` (default 4) skills, created on first use, hashed under `organisations`). Record this default in DEV_LOG; it lives in organisation state because `Npc` is frozen (F1) and POPULATION's general skill record is not built. If the lead prefers the association-value representation (SERVICES Q1 alternative), switch before C7a merges.
2. **`Treat` (107)**: the patient (or a carer leading it) gestures `NeedHelp` at a believed healer, or the healer sees visible signs; a request opens through C6's `Services.Open` (informal: in the provider's mind; organised: in the org ring); the healer within `Reach.Touch` for `TreatTicks` performs `Tend` plus skill and an optional remedy lot (`Goods.Consume(..., ConsumeReason.Treatment)`). Effects exactly per SERVICES §5.1: severity loss × `TendSeverityPermille × (1000 − Skill × SkillGain/1000 − Remedy)/1000` floored at `TreatFloorPermille` (300); injury recovery × `(2000 + Skill × InjuryGain/1000 + Remedy)` permille. **With skill 0 and no remedy, `Treat` equals `Tend`.** Treatment changes the patient through the existing `Life.TendedDay`/injury fields via the C7 hook into the M4 path; no faction or organisation modifier exists.
3. **Diagnosis** `SeenNeed` = perceived display level + noise falling with skill (draw label `svc-diagnose (request, tick)`).
4. **Healing skill growth** with each completed treatment (more if the patient recovers).
5. **Herbs**: gathered at existing `Roots` sources as an alternate yield of a C7 gather act (`Goods.Produce(..., ProduceReason.Foraged)` of `Ware.Herbs`), because `SourceKind` has no `Herbs` value and adding one is a frozen-enum change. If a distinct source is needed, ask the lead for an amendment (`SourceKind.Herbs = 6`).
6. **Providers**: private healer (fees by gesture, valued by each side) and a **healer's house template** on C6 (communal or private, `Healer` office, `Treat` service kind, beds = patients resting at the house). Founding by C6's opportunity path from need signals (injuries seen untreated, tending given).
7. `TreatmentStarted`/`TreatmentEnded` events; `HealthCard` rows in the person card (treatments received and given).

**C7b education:**
8. **`Demonstrate`** (gesture 21, act within sight): the learner attending gets a vicarious observation of the activity and its consequence (`Origin.Vicarious` entries through the store's public API).
9. **`Teach` (108) / `Attend` (109)** (gesture 18): teacher and learner within gesture range for `TeachTicks`; the teacher's top associations for the topic's situations are copied to the learner as `Origin.Imitated` entries with fidelity `(TeacherSkill × Attention)/1000` and value scaled by the learner's trust in the teacher; the learner's topic skill rises by `TeachSkillGain × fidelity/1000`, capped below the teacher's. Wrong associations are copied as they are.
10. `Learning/Imitation.cs` (new, C7-owned): the copy routine using **only** `AssociationStore`/`Learner` public members. Any need to change `Learner` → amendment row first.
11. `Attend` is a candidate in the learner's mind (it chooses to attend); `MaxLearnersPerSession` 4.
12. Apprenticeship role (optional, last): an `Apprentice` role on C3/C6 records; supervision bonus while the master is in sight.
