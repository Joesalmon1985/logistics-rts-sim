## 1. Objective
Processed and raw industrial goods change hands **only through existing mechanisms**: a refinery or smelter transforms (I1) and **earns only when someone actually pays** for what it hands over. I3 adds the one missing record, the **`SupplyAgreement`** between two owners, and the trace that ties a delivery's ownership change to its payment: **`IndustrialSaleClosed`**. There is **no external buyer, no price table, no market sink, no conversion of ore into credits** (D6.2; the first brief's buyer has no successor, contracts §22.11 #34). Agreements are made by persons, deliveries are physical unloads, and every delivery opens a C2 obligation that only a payment settles.

## 2. User-facing outcome
In Iron Coast, the mining company's office-holder and the cooperative smelter's office-holder agree, face to face, that the miners deliver 20 iron ore every two days for an agreed number of coins. Each unload at the smelter makes the ore the smelter's and opens a debt; the smelter's paymaster later walks over and hands the coins to the miners' agent, closing the sale. If the smelter never pays, the debt stays open and nothing enforces it; the miners may stop delivering. The god sees these agreements on the organisation cards (I6) and can only influence the people who make or keep them.

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **`SupplyAgreement` record** (`Industry/Trade/Agreements.cs`, `IndustryState.Trade.Agreements`, ascending `AgreementId`, only allocator `Agreements.Make`): IND §4.9 fields **plus `Kind`** (`Supply = 1`, `Electricity = 2` for I4, `Procurement = 3` for I5, `Haulage = 4` later) and `PriceTerm { Kind: Goods | Money | None, Unit (ware or MoneyId when K2b exists), CountPerUnit }`; `Status` Active, Suspended, Ended, Breached; counters `Delivered`, `Paid`, `OpenObligations` (truth; the orgs' own records may differ). One API for all kinds so I4 and I5 never allocate agreement ids themselves.
2. **Agreeing** is two decisions, one per party, each by an office-holder (C6 `DecisionRule`; or the NPC owner itself) who **perceives** the other party's offer (M5 `Offer` gesture with topic `Order`/ware, or a message relayed by members; no remote contracting). `Agreements.Make` is called only when both acceptances exist; `SupplyAgreementMade`. A candidate for proposing and accepting enters the office-holder's own score (Industry quota), weighed by its own valuation; no rule forces agreement.
3. **Deliveries**: an unload into the buyer's `DeliverToBin` by the seller's person or vehicle (I2's unload act, or a hand-carried `Deposit`) under an active agreement passes ownership at the unload: `Goods.Move(..., OwnerRule.Set to buyer, OwnershipBasis.SupplyAgreement)`, and **opens exactly one C2 obligation** of source `Supply` (debtor buyer, creditor seller, `PerDelivery × price term`); `SupplyDelivered`. Without C2's `Supply` source (K2a not merged) a delivery under a **money** price term is refused at agreement time (`no_obligation_source`) and only goods-for-goods agreements run; record the fallback.
4. **Recorded sale** (slice step 4, sale branch): when the buyer's person pays (K2a `Payments` with purpose `Sale`, or a goods hand-over back with basis `Purchase`/`Exchanged` when no money exists), and the obligation settles, emit **`IndustrialSaleClosed { Agreement or Deal, Seller, Buyer, Ware, Count, PaymentRef }`** linking the `OwnershipChanged` rows and the payment. A spot sale outside an agreement (an M5 deal between an organisation's agent and a buyer) gets the same trace with `Deal` set. **A delivery whose payment has not come is an open obligation, not revenue.**
5. **Breach and end**: either side may stop delivering or paying by its own choice; at the day start your partial checks schedules (`EveryDays`) and records a missed delivery or a payment overdue past the agreement's grace as `SupplyAgreementBreached` (recorded, **never enforced**); `SupplyAgreementEnded` by `EndDay` or by either party's decision. No automatic delivery, payment, penalty or termination.
6. **Orders under an agreement**: the seller's office-holder places matching production orders with `Order.Agreement` set (I1's verb); you never write into a queue.
7. Content for Q3: the `ind_slice` agreement mining company → smelter (iron ore, coin price) and mining company → utility (coal); your own scenario `DotNet/God.Runner/Scenarios/i3_trade.scenario.json`.
8. Migration **`0033_industrial_trade.sql`** and `I3Mappings`.

## 18. Risks
- **A hidden market.** Any "default price", automatic buyer or auto-settlement is a D6.2 violation; the static test and T8 guard it.
- **K2a timing.** Without K2a there is no `Supply` obligation source and no money payment: run goods-for-goods only and report `VS4_OreSold` with money as *not run*.
- **Agreement kinds.** IND §4.9's struct has no `Kind`, but contracts §22.4 give one `AgreementId` space to supply, electricity, procurement and haulage agreements, and IMIL §3.3 specialises by `Kind = Procurement`. Default taken: a `Kind` field on the one record; tell I4 and I5 to call `Agreements.Make` (never their own record type).
- **Remote contracting.** Both acceptances must come from perception; never accept on behalf of an absent party.
