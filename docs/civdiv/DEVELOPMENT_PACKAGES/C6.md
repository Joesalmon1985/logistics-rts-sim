## 1. Objective
Organisations **emerge from individuals' need evidence** (D5.16) with several ownership models on one framework (D5.17). Service requests reach an organisation and are delivered by NPC labour (end-to-end scenario 4). The first template is the **communal food store** founded by two households, with a keeper office and a ration policy (welfare). Every organisation decision has a living decider; every material effect has an NPC actor within reach.

## 2. User-facing outcome
In an "institutional" scenario the player sees two households that keep running short gather, gesture an agreement and found a shared store; members deposit food, a keeper hands rations to a hungry member; an "institution formed" alert and an organisation card (records as the organisation wrote them, kept apart from divine observation; drawn by U5). Checkpoint E: "several simple institutions deliver actual services through NPC labour and resources" (with C7).

## 5. Allowed scope
**C6a (interim, unblocks C7):**
1. `Orgs.Enabled` behaviour (`OrgModule.StartGame` creates `GodState.Organisations` when on with a population) and `Orgs.Emergence` (0 = only scenario-seeded organisations).
2. **Organisation record** per ORGANISATIONS §3.2, first-version subset: `Id`, `Type`, `Purposes ≤ 4`, `Charter` (version, membership rule, `DecisionRule`, distribution, store access, dissolution rule), `Ownership`, `Shares ≤ 16`, `Faction`, `Offices ≤ 8`, `Roles ≤ 64`, `Assets ≤ 8`, `Stores ≤ 4`, `Ledger` (day in/out by cause), `Budget ≤ 8`, `Policies ≤ 16`, `Records` ring ≤ 512, `Requests` ring ≤ 256, `Decisions` ring ≤ 64, `Status`, `Founded`, `Founders ≤ 8`, `OriginOpportunity`. Relations, merger and split fields exist but stay unused.
3. **`Orgs.Found(...)`** (the only `OrgId` allocator), roles, offices with powers, `DecisionRule` = `OwnerDecides`, `HeadDecides`, `MajorityPresent` (votes counted by the counter as it saw them; ties keep the old state; below quorum lapses), the daily **decision slot** `(dayStart + OrgId × 7) % TicksPerDay`, `OrgDecision` records, policies as records, dormancy (`DormantDays` 5) and dissolution by `DissolutionRule` (`SetOwner(..., Divided)` in place; obligations orphaned through C2's API if merged).
4. **Opportunity and founding** (§4.1–4.2): `OrgTemplate` data table (`Orgs/OrgTemplates` inside `Orgs.cs` or a sibling owned file); `OpportunityBelief ≤ 2` per NPC from **own experience** need signals; support beliefs (`SocialView.Trust ≥ SupportTrust`, default 300); the `ProposeOrg` candidate in the Orgs quota; proposal by `AskJoin` with `TopicKind.Org`; answers by each listener's own scores; the founding meeting (minimum founders present within gesture range, each signals `Accept` again) → `Orgs.Found`. Availability makes a candidate possible; it **creates nothing** (ORG-INV-6).
5. **Membership**: `Join` (104), `LeaveOrg` (105) per membership rule; death ends roles (`OrgHooks.OnDeath`); a resurrected NPC regains nothing.
6. **`OrgMind`** (`NpcMind.Orgs`, ≤ 6 `OrgBelief` ~144 B: `OrgId`, my role, believed head, believed place and store node, known policies, my terms as understood, trust in the org, confidence, tick; plus ≤ 2 `OpportunityBelief`).
7. **Service model** (INSTITUTIONAL_SERVICES §3): `ServiceKind`, `ServiceRequest` (in the org ring, or ≤ 4 informal in the provider's mind), `Services.Open` (the only `RequestId` allocator), queue policies first-come and most-severe-seen, provider choice, `ServiceActivity` as the provider's ordinary intent with the request id as purpose ref, outcome by the owning system's state change, record by a role-holder. `RequestService` (106) and gesture 22. Capacity and recorded unmet demand per org per day; true unmet demand is telemetry only.
8. **Records** (`Orgs/OrgRecords.cs`): written only by a role-holder's `WriteRecord` act at the org's place; what the writer knows; can be late or wrong; with no recorder nothing is written.
9. **`OrganisationSystem` (260)**: day start (status updates, request expiry, `OrgDay` snapshot) and the per-tick service queue upkeep, with **partial-method slots** for C7, C8, C9, C10 in fixed order. `OrgHooks`/`OrgMindHooks` dispatcher bodies with partials for C7–C10.
10. **`InstitutionDirector` (905)**, host-attached like `AiDirector`: at each org's decision slot, for each living, awake office-holder with `Propose`, builds `InstitutionView` and calls the seated `IInstitutionPolicy` (default `MemberUtilityPolicy`; `RuleFollowerPolicy` as the benchmark control) whose proposals are C6's own **org command types** (plain `Command`s, never `FactionCommand`; validated: the actor holds an office with the power) submitted in-step.
11. **`InstitutionView` rewritten** (`Policy/Observations.cs`): `OrgId`, `DeciderNpcId`, office and powers, the records the decider may read, the requests it knows of, budget and store counts **as last recorded**, the decider's own `MindView`, tick. Built only from layer 4 and the decider's mind (I-D5-1).
12. **`Orgs.RelayRecipients(org, act)`**: office-holders whose powers cover the act in ascending NpcId, plus members present at the meeting place for a meeting rule (D2 emits `InstructionRelayed` and delivers; C10 uses it for governments).
13. **`Permits.Believed` basis `OrgRole`** from `OrgBelief` and the believed `StoreAccess` (a query only).
14. `OrgCard.Fill` (`Information/PeopleCards.Orgs.cs`): memberships, roles, believed terms; and the organisation card data (records view, layer 4) for U5.
15. Pin **P5** `Orgs_NoEmergence_Neutral`.

**C6b (communal store and welfare, C8's Tier 2 slots):**
16. Template **shared food store** (communal, `MinFounders` 2, membership `HouseholdOnly`/`ByAcceptance`, `StoreAccess = MembersByRation`, `Keeper` and `Recorder` offices, `MajorityPresent` for policy changes, `HeadDecides` for routine); store bin `BinKind.CommunalStore` owned `Org(id)`; deposits by `Store` with basis `OrgContribution`.
