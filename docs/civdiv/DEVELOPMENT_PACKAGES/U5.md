# U5: Reports and inspection

Package spec for the Civilisation and Divinity run (owner brief 26 Nov 2026 §40). Self-contained. Paths start at the repository root. "Contracts" = [SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md](../SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md) (binding). This is a **view package**: it reads views and cards only, labels every figure with its source layer, and never reads `GodState` from HUD types.

## 1. Identifier

**U5**, Tier 2, Checkpoints **C** (Economy tab) and **E** (life history, memorial, institutional panels). Effort about 3.5 agent-hours. Branch `unity/u5`, worktree `wt-lrs-u5`. Because its inputs land at different times, U5 is delivered in parts (U5a Economy, U5b life and memorial, U5c institutions and KPIs), each on a branch `unity/u5a`, `unity/u5b`, `unity/u5c` from the then-current `unity/poc`.

## 2. Objective

Divine analysis (Mode D, brief §6) and deeper inspection (brief §31) with **record versus observation shown side by side** (D5.28): an Economy tab (possessions, assets, employment, debts), the family tree, the life history and the memorial with the resurrection panel, institutional cards and a reports panel in which an institution's own record (layer 4) and the god's observation (layer 8) are two columns with a visible gap, and KPI info popovers for the Q1 subset.

## 3. User-facing outcome

The player opens a villager's Economy tab and sees what they carry and own, the assets they work, who employs them and what they owe; the Family tab draws parents, partners, adopted and fostered children and carers as four distinct link kinds; the Life tab is a timeline from birth with divine commands, compulsions, possessions (marked "divinely controlled"), miracles, deaths and resurrections; a dead villager has a memorial with their estate's fate and a resurrection panel (variants, costs, and the stated consequences: estates stay, partnerships are not restored). The reports panel shows, for example, "Records: 41 adults. Observed: 47." with the institution's name and as-of tick; every KPI has an info popover with its definition, period, denominator and limitations.

## 6. Allowed scope

**U5a (Checkpoint C, with C1):** an **Economy tab** (`GodHud.People.Economy.cs`) from `NpcInspection.Economy` (C1's `CivCard`): carried and owned goods with ownership basis, productive assets used or owned, use rights; employment and wages (C3's rows when they exist); debts and loans (C2's rows when they exist); each row labelled authoritative or "recorded by *org*" (INFORMATION §12.4). Missing packages show an honest "not tracked yet", never invented numbers.

**U5b (Checkpoint E, with D7):** **Family** tab (rows by generation; partnership bar, biological parenthood solid, adoption/fostering dashed, caregiving dotted; the dead dimmed with a cross, the resurrected with a ring; toggle "true genealogy" vs "as this person believes" with differences marked), **Life** tab (`GodHud.People.Life.cs`: a timeline built from the person's events available through the divine view: birth, partnerships, children, households, injuries and illnesses, divine instructions received, compulsions, possession periods marked divinely controlled, miracles, deaths with `LifeNo`, resurrections), and the **memorial** for a dead person (name, dates, cause, family links, the estate's fate from `Estate*` events, a "Remembered" list filterable by household, generation, cause) with the **resurrection panel** (archived or not, the three variants Lesser/Full/Restoration with cost previews from `Gate.Check`, the consequences stated; sends `Resurrect`).

**U5c (Checkpoint E, with C6; KPI popovers with Q1):** institutional cards (`NpcInspection.Orgs` / C6's records view: members, offices, services, requests and outcomes, stores) and a **reports panel** (`Assets/God/Game/Hud/Reports/`) with two columns on every figure: **institutional record** (green: named institution, as-of tick, coverage, its mistakes and delays) and **divine observation** (gold: what `IDivineView` shows now) plus a difference bar; never one merged number. Views: population and households, economy, institutions, religion (affiliation and citizenship as separate lines), divine (interventions, Mythic spent, responses). **KPI info popovers** for the Q1 subset (definition, period, denominator, source, limitations), reading the KPI definitions Q1 ships; no universal wellbeing score.

**Room for the industrial workstream** (I6: vehicle, power station, factory and mine inspection; production queues; electricity and logistics shortages; owner D6.19): the reports panel takes a list of report descriptors (title, source kind, two-column provider) and the inspection card takes a list of tab descriptors, so I6 adds "Vehicle", "Production" or "Grid" views without editing U5's drawing code (for example the power-station inspection of [ELECTRICITY_DESIGN.md](../../stage4/ELECTRICITY_DESIGN.md), which shows the utility's records beside the god's observation: the same two-column provider). Do not implement them.
