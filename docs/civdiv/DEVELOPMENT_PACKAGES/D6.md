# D6: Structured commandments, divine beliefs and minimal affiliation

Package spec for the Civilisation and Divinity run (owner brief 26 Nov 2026 §40). Self-contained. Paths are relative to `Assets/God/Sim/` unless they start with `Assets/`, `DotNet/` or `docs/`. "Contracts" = [SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md](../SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md) (binding).

## 1. Identifier

**D6**, Tier 1 (commandments) and Tier 2 (beliefs from evidence, prayer, affiliation). First shown in the build after its merge (planned: the C/D build); scenario 13 at Checkpoint F. Effort about 4 agent-hours. Branch `unity/d6`, worktree `wt-lrs-d6`.

## 2. Objective

A persistent, structured commandment is revealed by a god, learned, interpreted (sometimes wrongly), communicated person to person, and influences behaviour **without universal enforcement** (scenario 13; brief §12). Belief, faith, trust, fear and loyalty towards each god are five separate per-person fields (D5.15); religious affiliation is a separate spell record that never changes citizenship (scenario 16, together with C10).

## 3. User-facing outcome

The player picks a template such as "Share surplus food with hungry children" (or composes one from menus), reveals it to three believers; over days more villagers know it, some adopt it, some misunderstand it, some forget it. Adopters give food to begging children more often, but a hungry adopter may still refuse. The person card shows the five belief bars per god and what the person *thinks* the commandment says. Later (Tier 2), people pray when ill and learn whether prayer helps; a person can change religion without changing citizenship.

## 6. Allowed scope

**Tier 1 (commandments):**
- `Commandment` records in `DivinityState.Doctrine` (sorted by id, `NextCommandmentId`), with every brief §12 field (`Modality` per rule, verb, object class or ware, `Applies`, `Condition`, `Scope`, `IntroducedTick`, `God`, `Importance` 1-5, `Revision`, `History`, `Revoked`).
- `CommandmentRules.Validate` (pure; rejects empty, oversized or unknown ids with `bad_target`); `CommandmentTemplates` static table with templates **T1, T3, T4, T6, T7, T12** (the index's Tier 1 set) and **T11 (warn part only)** if trivial (the design lists it as Tier 1; note this in your report). T2, T5, T8-T10 present as data but matching nothing until their verbs exist.
- Handlers: `RevealCommandment { God, Template, Rules[], Importance, Recipients }` (delivered through **D2's path**: perception, decode with misunderstanding, a visible sign; witnesses at half salience), `ReviseCommandment` (keeps id, bumps `Revision`, history; only people told update), `RevokeCommandment` (told people move adoption towards rejection −600; the god cannot erase knowledge). Costs 25 / 10 / 5 Mythic via `Mythic.TrySpend`.
- Per-person knowledge in `DivineMind.Known` (cap 8, eviction lowest salience, ties by id): `KnownCommandment { Commandment, Revision, Source, FromNpc, Adoption, Salience, Interpretation }`; states and bands (heard, learned, adopted, rejected, forgotten); misunderstanding draws `commandment-interpret` (a dropped condition or a shifted verb); salience decay and forgetting (`CommandmentForgotten`).
- `DivinityMindHooks.D6.cs`: the **D6 part of `DivineTerm`** (`sign × ImportanceVp[Importance] × Adoption/1000 × Salience/1000 × (1000 + 2 × Conformity)/1000`, summed over matching rules of the person's *interpretation* from its own context, clamped ±2000) and the feature key `Feature.Divine(5|6|7)`; conditions evaluated only from the person's `ThinkScratch` (layers 2, 3, 6), never true state. Conflicts simply add. **Never removes, filters or blocks a candidate** (I-CR-1).
- Transmission: telling with `Say(Teach)` and `TopicKind.Commandment`, carrying the speaker's interpretation (not the original); the receiver decodes with M5 noise; a minimal household pass if the design's daily slot is cheap.
- `Information/CommandmentText.cs`: `CommandmentText.Render(rules, locale)` from phrase tables, **pure and one-way**; nothing parses text into rules; **no sim rule calls it** (static test I-CR-4 scans `Sim/` except `Information/CommandmentText.cs` and the card fillers that pass rendered strings to the UI).
- Belief rows on the Divine card (`PeopleCards.Divine.cs`, D6 part): five bars per god, affiliations, citizenship shown separately (from C5 when it exists), the person's interpretations with their state.

**Tier 2 (beliefs, prayer, affiliation):**
- `GodBelief` (five independent fields per god, `DivineMind.Gods`, cap = god count) updated only from the person's own perceptions (a perceived instruction, a witnessed miracle or smite, a miracle on itself, answered/unanswered prayer, others' communication) with cause refs in `BeliefChanged` (869, HV, always kept when |Δ| ≥ 100). Replace D2's default belief/trust tuning with real `GodBelief` reads (D2's term reads D6's fields through the `DivineMind` API).
- `Religion` records and founding religions (`ReligionFounded` 867), `Affiliation` spells (`AffiliationChanged` 868; several per person allowed) that **never** write C5 citizenship, `Npc.Faction` or `SocialMind` (I-CR-3).
- `Pray` (116), `Worship` (117), `MakeOffering` (118) verbs with their affordances and the gestures 19 and 20; `PrayerMade` (870, HV), `WorshipHeld` (871); prayer episodes (kind 37) so a person learns whether praying pays; `BinKind.Shrine` offerings bin at a D3 `ConsecratedPlace` (owned by the receiving organisation once C6 exists, or offerings consumed with `ConsumeReason.Offering`).
