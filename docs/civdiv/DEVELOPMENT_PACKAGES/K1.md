## 1. Objective
The explicit **knowledge ontology** of D6.9–D6.11, with every distinction kept: individual knowledge (levels and fidelity in a person's mind), formal designs (data-driven `DesignDef` envelopes around the industrial payloads), physical documents (unique `Document` lots with an artefact record), ownership of those documents (ordinary goods ownership), patents and licences (legal claims recognised per government, never physical blocks), trade secrets (only concealment) and practical ability (skills). **Knowledge copies without loss to its holder** (KN-I1). **Only knowledge and physical inputs gate production; a right never does** (R33, IA-3): unlicensed use is classified as `UnauthorisedDesignUse` † and may become a dispute through people.

## 2. User-facing outcome
In Iron Coast, the harvester can be driven only by people who learned to drive it, and the factory builds a harvester only when a worker present knows the design or follows a manual at the site. An engineer teaches an apprentice and keeps what she knows; a clerk copies the factory's manual and the original is unchanged; a reader learns from the copy. The patent office registers the harvester patent; the factory owner buys a licence; a reckless workshop builds the design without one and is not stopped — but when the patent holder's officer walks past and sees it, he may complain at the commercial court. The god may reveal knowledge (Tier 3), which confers no patent. Inspection shows "knows", "owns a document", "holds a right" and "has built" as four different lines.
