## 1. Objective
Mines and factories with **production orders, finite capacity, material inputs, workers and electricity requirements, and completed physical products** (D6.5), on **one recipe table and one runner** shared with the population economy (IND-3): finite geological deposits (D6.3), facilities as C1 productive assets with physical bins, `RecipeBook` (rows 1–20 generated from the stage 3 workshop defs, 21+ industrial), the recipe runner at system order 264, production orders placed by persons, and waste as an ordinary output (D6.13, waste part).

## 2. User-facing outcome
In an industrial scenario (`Industry.Enabled = 1`), a mine on a seeded iron-ore deposit turns real worker labour into ore lots that wait in its output bin; a smelter consumes delivered ore and coal and puts out steel plus slag and ash; a factory with workers, inputs and power assembles a harvester from steel, parts, an engine and wire. Each facility knows why it is idle (missing inputs, no workers, no power, output full, no one knows the design). Nothing produces money. The player sees it once I6 draws it (facility card: owner, workers, queue with progress, stall reason); until then the headless scenario is the demonstration.
