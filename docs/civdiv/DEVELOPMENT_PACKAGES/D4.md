# D4: Embodiment: possession (D4a) and manifested avatar (D4b)

Package spec for the Civilisation and Divinity run (owner brief 26 Nov 2026 §40). Self-contained. Paths are relative to `Assets/God/Sim/` unless they start with `Assets/`, `DotNet/` or `docs/`. "Contracts" = [SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md](../SHARED_ARCHITECTURAL_CONTRACTS.md) (binding). D4 **absorbs the brief's D5** (avatar): it owns D5's contract slots (`DivinityState.D5.cs`, codes 850-859, migration `0011`; index §0).

## 1. Identifier

**D4**, Tier 2 (first in the Tier 2 priority order: D4a possession, later D4b avatar), Checkpoint **D** (Personal divine interaction). Effort about 5 agent-hours (D4a 2.5, D4b 2.5). Branch `unity/d4`, worktree `wt-lrs-d4`.

## 2. Objective

- **D4a, possession (Mode C):** the player takes control of a living person, performs real actions through the existing primitives, and returns control to the same individual (scenario 10; D5.8, D5.10). The body continues; the mind stays conscious and may learn; every act is recorded as divinely controlled; Mythic is charged continuously; one possession per god.
- **D4b, avatar (Mode B):** the player manifests a distinct persistent divine body that walks with the ordinary movement system, handles goods, gestures, can be attacked, and returns the god to divine existence when destroyed (scenario 11; D5.9, D5.14).

## 3. User-facing outcome

Possession: the player picks a villager, the camera moves behind them (U1b), WASD steers them node by node, the player picks up a log and gives it to a neighbour, then releases; the villager goes on with its own life, remembering the experience, and the neighbour remembers a gift from that villager. If Mythic runs out or the villager starves, the possession ends at once. Avatar: a luminous figure appears, walks through the village, picks up a fish and gives it to a hungry child, performs gestures people watch and learn from; if struck down it dissolves and the god is unharmed.

## 6. Allowed scope

**D4a, possession:**
- `BeginPossession { God, Npc }`: preconditions (living person of any faction; `Possession.AllowForeign` default on in sandbox; not possessed by another god `already_controlled`; `MaxPerGod = 1` → `control_limit`; biological age ≥ `Possession.MinAgeMilli` 0.5 years; Mythic); opens a `ControlEntry { Kind = Possessed }` via `Control.TryAcquire` and a `PossessionSpell`; supersedes a compulsion on that body and dismisses the god's own avatar (`Superseded`); `PossessionStarted` (845).
- `EndPossession { God }` (`Cancelled`); end on `MythicExhausted` (upkeep 0.05 Mythic per tick via D1), `Death` (`DivinityHooks.D4.cs` `OnDeath` releases first), `Superseded`; `PossessionEnded` (846) exactly once (CT1); `ForceThink` the next tick so the person's own policy decides afresh.
- `DivineControlInput` for a possessed body is accepted (handler in F1's `Control.cs`; D4 provides the spell-existence check and the D4 `TryAct` partial for `Kind = Action` acts via `DivineActs.Map`). No policy draw while possessed (`RngPolicy` not advanced).
- `PossessedActionStarted` (847, HV) with the intervention; action events carry `Control = 2`; `DecisionRecord.Control = Possessed`.
- Learning: traces carry `Feature.Divine(Possessed 4)`, scaled by `Possession.LearnPermille` (1000); afterwards `SelfFeature.Possessed` (51) for `MemoryDays` (2); `BlameShiftPermille` (700) applied to the social evidence of a victim who perceived the possession (`SituationKind.DivineSign`).
- `PossessionView` (read-only struct of `IDivineView`, filled by `DivineViewFiller` from `ControlTable`: npc, god, started tick, upkeep per tick, ticks left at current balance) as an additive `IDivineView` member (contracts 19.2).

**D4b, avatar (the brief's D5):**
- First `ManifestAvatar { God, Node }` creates the body record once: `Npc` with `Kind = Avatar`, `God`, id `AvatarBodyId = 0x4000_0000 + GodId`, in `PopulationState.Npcs` (sorted, so `Find` works), `NpcMind` with `LearningMode.Off`, no `Life`, no household, no hut rights, `Policy = PolicyRegistry.None`; reused by every later manifestation. Manifested: `Alive = true` at a land node in the god's view; ends the god's possession (`Superseded`); cost 20, upkeep 0.02 Mythic per tick; an `AvatarSpell`; `AvatarManifested` (850).
- `DismissAvatar { God }` (`Cancelled`) and dismissal on `MythicExhausted`: carried lots to the ground with `MoveReason.AvatarDrop` (ownership unchanged), `Alive = false`, `AvatarDismissed` (851).
- No biology (F1's `Body()` skip): no hunger, fatigue, cold, ageing, illness, sleep. Damage via `Bodies.Damage` → D4's D5 hook: `AvatarDamaged` (853) per blow; at `Health <= 0` **destroyed, not killed**: no `NpcDied`, no estate, no `Kill`; lots dropped with `AvatarDrop`; `AvatarDestroyed` (852); spell ends `Destroyed`; cooldown `Divinity.Cooldown.Avatar` 600 ticks; health restored at next manifestation. `Bodies.Heal` from the god's own `HealInjury` heals it.
- Movement and actions via `DivineControlInput` exactly as possession (`ControlKind.AvatarBody`), same `WalkTo` step costs; goods with `OwnerRule.Keep` on pick-up (an owned lot stays owned: C1 later classifies theft) and `ToReceiver` basis `Gift` on give; an avatar never becomes an owner; gestures through `Communicate` acts and M5 delivery (listeners decode with ordinary misunderstanding).
- Perception: `SeenPerson.Body = Avatar`, `NpcGroup.Avatar` (10), `SituationKind.AvatarInSight` (81); recognition is D6 belief (no automatic recognition, contracts 21.3). Observers counted per spell; demonstration reaches observers through ordinary vicarious learning where it exists.
- `AvatarActionStarted` (854, HV); action events carry `Control = 3`. `AvatarView` additive `IDivineView` member (body, node, health, carry, alive, upkeep).

## 7. Excluded scope

- Several possessions at once, avatar plus possession together (one embodied focus per god, contracts 21.2), possessing animals (`BodyKind` 2+ reserved).
- **Later extension, not in this run: possession of vehicle operators** (owner D6.4, D6.8: vehicles are persistent assets operated by NPCs). Nothing in D4 may assume a possessed body is on foot forever: keep the movement step behind `Control`'s node choice and `WalkTo`, and keep `PossessionSpell` keyed by the person (not by a movement mode), so that a later industrial package can let a possessed operator drive a vehicle through that vehicle's own operating actions. The industrial design draft [VEHICLES_AND_HARVESTING_DESIGN.md §4.7 The god and vehicles](../../stage4/VEHICLES_AND_HARVESTING_DESIGN.md) already plans this on top of D4 unchanged (a possessed driver's `Heading`/`MoveTo` drives the vehicle, which follows the body; `DivineAct` 22 Board and 23 Alight added to D2's table; every act `Control = 2`; ownership of vehicle and cargo unchanged). Do not add vehicle code or fields.
- Avatar `Teach` and `Demonstrate` as C7 verbs (Tier 3; only ordinary acts and the gestures that exist). Combat verbs to attack the avatar (C4); D4 only receives damage through `Bodies.Damage` (tested with a direct damage call or an existing contest).
- Cameras and input sampling (U1b), pills and mode switcher (U2b), effects (U3b).
- Any edit to `Control.cs`, `PopulationSystem.cs`, `Bodies.cs`, `PopulationModule.Kill`.
