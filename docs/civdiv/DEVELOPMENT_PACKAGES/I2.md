## 1. Objective
A **persistent NPC operates a harvester** that **collects physical ore** and **delivers it** to a smelter, and coal to a power station; a newly built vehicle is boarded and driven by an existing NPC; an empty fuel depot stops the harvester (D6.4; slice steps 1, 2, 3, 5 (delivery), 8, 9a). A vehicle is a **C1 productive asset, never an `Npc`** (VEH-1, IA-4): it moves only by its living driver's step, carries goods in ordinary bins, burns physical fuel, wears, and is operated by people who may follow, abandon or misuse its routine.

## 2. User-facing outcome
In Iron Coast, a named miner with an employment contract walks to the harvester, boards it and drives the OpenRA-style loop: hill deposit → load ore → smelter → unload → back, eating at the roadside when hungry. The god can instruct, compel or (with D4a) possess the operator. Once I6 lands, the harvester glides between nodes with its cargo visibly heaped and its vehicle card shows owner, operator, routine step, cargo, tank and condition.

Seated persons do only vehicle verbs, gestures and talk (eating from carry while parked allowed); their own movement intents are refused `in_vehicle`.
4. **Driving** (verb 22, `NpcVerb` 202): `PopulationSystem` walks the **driver**; `StepTicks` returns the vehicle's road/off-road ticks and refuses impassable nodes; `PathCosts` passes the vehicle's cost profile to the unchanged `NodePather`; `Moved` moves the vehicle node, `PrevNode`, every bin by **`Goods.MoveBin`** (actor = driver; the only caller), every other occupant (`Npc.Node = to`, path cleared), accrues `FuelMilliPerNode` and burns whole units with `Consume(TankBin, lot, 1, Combusted, actor = driver)`, adds wear, increments odometer and `NodesSinceService`, emits `VehicleMoved` (HV). Before a step, an uncoverable step is refused: status `OutOfFuel`, `VehicleStopped { Reason = Fuel }`, the driver's intent fails as a physical failure.

## 16. Performance considerations
Vehicle steps happen inside the existing driver step (no extra pathing per tick; one `NodePather` call per drive with the vehicle's profile, the same expansion cap). `Moved` costs O(bins + occupants) per step. The occupancy lookup is a dictionary index (`[JsonIgnore]`, never enumerated for decisions; iterate `Vehicles.List` in ascending order where order matters). Report `PopulationSystem` µs per tick on `i2_harvest` with 2 and with 10 vehicles.
