## 1. Objective
- A **government is an organisation of NPCs** (D5.2): per faction, a Head and a small council on C6's framework. A divine instruction addressed to it is **relayed to its office-holders**, each of whom perceives it individually; the government **adopts, modifies or refuses** it through its own `IInstitutionPolicy` and decision rule (end-to-end scenario 15).
- **Citizenship** is a record separate from religious affiliation and faction of origin, so an affiliation change never changes citizenship (scenario 16).
- One **temple** template (religious organisation) and **law adoption** that copies a commandment's rules at a revision.

## 2. User-facing outcome
The player instructs a government ("fund a healer with 5 berries a day"); the institution-scope response shows each councillor's response and the government's recorded decision: refused, modified (a smaller grant) or adopted and then carried out only when an official actually hires and pays a healer. An NPC who converts to another god keeps its citizenship line unchanged on the Faith tab (data in the cards; panels by U5/U2b).

## 5. Allowed scope
1. **Government template** on C6: `OrgType.Government`, `OwnershipModel.State`, `Faction = f`, purposes `Govern` (and `Defend` until a military org exists); offices `Head`, `Councillor` × up to 3, `Treasurer`; rules `HeadDecides` for routine and `MajorityPresent` among councillors for laws, budgets and founding services (`Council` proper is Tier 3: implement it as `MajorityPresent` restricted to councillor offices, recorded as a default). Seeded at start by **`Orgs.C10.SeedGovernments`** (default **0**, so pin P5 "no seeds" holds; god-only and C10 scenarios set it to 1): the 3 oldest adults of each faction's founders as councillors, the most trusted of them as Head. A scenario may start with none; any community can later found one from the "government council" template through C6's opportunity path.
2. **`OrgState.GovernmentOf(faction)`**: resolves `OwnerKind.State` reads to `Org(gov)` when a government exists; new C10 code writes `Org(gov)`. Existing stage 3 `State(f)` lots and HQ stocks are **not** rewritten in this run (the alias stays, listed as a temporary mechanism); record that in DEV_LOG.
3. **Relayed instructions**: an instruction targeted at a government (`DivineScope.Org`) is relayed by D2 to `Orgs.RelayRecipients(gov, act)`; each recipient perceives it through its own perception (D2). At the government's decision slot the deciding office-holder's `InstitutionView` includes the **instructions it perceived** (from its own mind) and its own god beliefs if D6 is present (layer 6). The government's policy (`MemberUtilityPolicy` as itself) proposes: **ignore**, **refuse** (a visible refusal gesture), **adopt** as its own proposal (`OrgDecision.Source = DivineInstruction`), or **adopt modified** (changed arguments: a smaller grant, a later date). The decision rule then applies (councillors vote by their own interests and beliefs). Material effects still need an official's physical action (hire, hand over), possibly refused. `GovernmentDecision` (592) records the outcome as counted. **No divine code changes a government record directly.**
   - If `InstitutionView` lacks a field for perceived instructions or god beliefs, ask the lead for an **additive** member through C6's owner (the view is C6's file); do not fork it.
4. **Compulsion and possession of officials** need no C10 code: they act through the individual (contracts §11); C10 tests that a compelled Head's proposal does not change councillors' votes (ORG-ACC-15c).
5. **Minimal citizenship (C5)** in `CivState.C5`: `CitizenshipSpell { int Npc; int Polity /*OrgId of a Government, or FactionId + flag until one exists*/; long FromTick, ToTick; CitizenshipBasis Basis (Founder, Birth, Immigration, Naturalised, Emigrated, Died) }`, ascending (Npc, FromTick); one open spell per living person; opened at start for founders, at birth (a parent's polity), on immigration (the joined faction's polity); closed at death. Changes only by these events and the C5 API (`Citizenship.Change`); `CitizenshipChanged` (290). Requires `Civ.Enabled = 1`.
6. **Separation guarantees**: no code path changes citizenship, government office or loyalty because of a religious role or affiliation, and no god id is ever a role-holder (ORG-I9 static check over `Sim/Orgs`, `Sim/Civ`, `Sim/Divinity`).
7. **Temple template** (needs D6): `OrgType.Religious`, `OwnershipModel.Religious` (one share held by `Org(id)`), offices `Priest` (head), `Keeper`, `Recorder`; a `Doctrine` policy listing commandment ids it teaches (may differ from what its god revealed); offerings as hand-overs into its `OrgStore`. Membership is a C6 role; **religious affiliation remains D6's** spell and is never set by joining or leaving a temple.
8. **Laws**: `Law { int Id; int Org; int SourceCommandment; int Revision; CommandmentRule[] Rules (copied); EnforcementKind Enforcement (None in this run) }` in `OrgState.C10`; `LawAdopted` (590) by a government decision under its rule, copying D6's rules at the current revision; later revisions do not change the law; `LawRepealed` (591). Laws are believed norms only (no enforcement until C9).
9. Scenarios `DotNet/God.Runner/Scenarios/org_gov_refusal.scenario.json` (scenario 15 fixtures: low-trust Head, high-trust Head with varied councillors) and `org_faith_citizenship.scenario.json` (scenario 16). (Q3 owns the final `civdiv_s15`/`s16` files; give them these as sources.)
